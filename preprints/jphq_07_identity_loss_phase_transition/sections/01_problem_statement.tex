\section{Problem Statement}

This paper addresses a single, frozen research question within the Ontology of
Continua and the executable specification ETS.K$_{\mathrm{EA}}$.

\paragraph{Frozen Question (Q7).}
Can enterprise identity loss be formalized as a phase transition rather than as
organizational failure in enterprises modeled as continua $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$?

The motivation for this question is structural. Existing accounts typically
conflate loss of enterprise identity with organizational failure, collapse, or
dysfunction. This conflation obscures the possibility that identity-supporting
structure may disappear while the enterprise continuum remains admissible and
operational.

The objective of this paper is to determine whether identity loss admits a
precise formal characterization as a phase transition internal to
$K_{\mathrm{EA}}$, distinct from collapse of $\Omega_{\mathrm{EA}}$ or vanishing
of $k_{\mathrm{EA}}$. No additional questions are posed, and no extensions of the
formal framework are introduced.

Accordingly, all results are evaluated solely with respect to their ability to
answer Q7. Phenomena not bearing directly on this question are intentionally
excluded.
