\section{Formal setting (OC/ETS-only)}
\label{sec:formal}

This paper takes the Ontology of Continua (OC Core) and the executable
specification \artifact{ETS.K_EA.v1.1} as immutable inputs
(\artifact{status: executable\_closed}). No symbols, operators, predicates,
relations, or semantics beyond those explicitly defined in OC Core or ETS are
introduced, extended, or reinterpreted. All references to structure, phase,
continuity, or transition are understood strictly within this fixed formal
vocabulary.

\paragraph{Enterprise continuum.}
An enterprise is modeled as a continuum $K_{EA}$ with admissible state region
$\Omega(K_{EA})$ and boundary $\partial\Omega(K_{EA})$. Each state
$s \in \Omega(K_{EA})$ is evaluated exclusively through ETS-defined variables,
predicates, and invariants. States outside $\Omega(K_{EA})$ are treated as
inadmissible and are not classified.

\paragraph{Axes.}
The set of axes $A$ is fixed by ETS and grouped into the following dimensions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Structural:} \artifact{coupling}, \artifact{modularity},
        \artifact{centralization}, \artifact{redundancy};
  \item \emph{Governance:} \artifact{decision\_latency},
        \artifact{rule\_enforceability}, \artifact{escalation\_integrity};
  \item \emph{Operations:} \artifact{delivery\_coherence},
        \artifact{incident\_recovery\_loop}, \artifact{change\_lead\_time};
  \item \emph{Resources:} \artifact{budget\_flexibility},
        \artifact{skills\_coverage}, \artifact{sup\-plier\_dependency};
  \item \emph{Information:} \artifact{observability},
        \artifact{traceability}, \artifact{semantic\_alignment};
  \item \emph{Risk:} \artifact{risk\_visibility}, \artifact{control\_gap\_rate}.
\end{itemize}
Axes are descriptive coordinates only; no metric structure, optimization
direction, or control affordance is assumed.

\paragraph{Structural tension and thresholds.}
Structural tension is represented by ETS-defined components
\[
f_k \in \{f_{\text{exist}}, f_{\text{stab}}, f_{\text{inertia}},
          f_{\text{collapse}}, f_{\text{stop}}\}.
\]
Each component is associated with a corresponding threshold
\[
\Theta_k \in \{\Theta_{\text{exist}}, \Theta_{\text{stab}},
               \Theta_{\text{inertia}}, \Theta_{\text{collapse}},
               \Theta_{\text{stop}}\}.
\]
Threshold crossings are evaluated strictly as logical predicates on states
$s \in \Omega(K_{EA})$. No temporal evolution law, causal mechanism, or control
interpretation is implied.

\paragraph{Cycles.}
ETS requires the presence of cycles
\[
C = \{\artifact{governance}, \artifact{value}, \artifact{reproduction},
      \artifact{finance}, \artifact{learning}\},
\]
with closure defined by graph-closed paths and recurrent patterns over the
ETS-defined time parameter $\tau$. Cycle absence, degradation, or breakage is
evaluated exclusively via ETS closure rules and invariants.

\paragraph{Continuity measure.}
The continuity (viability) measure $k_{EA} \in [0,1]$ is defined by the ETS
factorization
\[
k_{EA}
= \prod \{ H_{\Omega}, S_{\text{cycles}}, S_{\text{obs}},
           S_{\text{parent}}, S_{\text{thresholds}} \},
\]
with a hard-zero condition enforced whenever $H_{\Omega}=\text{false}$. The
quantity $k_{EA}$ is interpreted solely as a diagnostic indicator of admissible
continuity and not as a utility, fitness, or optimization target.

\paragraph{Phase logic and precedence.}
ETS defines a phase logic with strict precedence ordering
\[
\texttt{STOP} \succ \texttt{COLLAPSE} \succ
\texttt{INERTIA} \succ \texttt{SUCCESS}.
\]
Phase membership is determined solely by ETS predicates and evaluated pointwise
for each $s \in \Omega(K_{EA})$. Precedence resolves simultaneous predicate
satisfaction without implying recoverability or directionality.

\paragraph{Invariants and no-go constraints.}
All reasoning is constrained by ETS invariants:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \artifact{CycleInvariance};
  \item \artifact{EnergyOfTransformation};
  \item \artifact{ObservabilityInvariant};
  \item \artifact{ParentCompatibility};
  \item \artifact{TimeScaleInvariant}.
\end{itemize}
and by explicit no-go rules:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \artifact{NoFreeTransformation};
  \item \artifact{MetricSubstitution};
  \item \artifact{GovernanceSimulation};
  \item \artifact{InstantRecovery};
  \item \artifact{ExternalOverride}.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Scope of use.}
Within this paper, the formal setting is used exclusively for diagnostic
classification of states and phase relations. No optimization objective,
intervention strategy, steering logic, or governance prescription is inferred or
implied.
