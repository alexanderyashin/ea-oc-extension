\section{Falsifiability conditions}
\label{sec:falsify}

The claims of this paper are falsified if any of the following conditions holds.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Absence of threshold-induced discontinuities.}
  If all ETS.K\_EA.v1.1 phase predicates can be reformulated as continuous,
  precedence-free conditions such that phase classification varies continuously
  with respect to admissible state parametrizations, then the proposed analogy
  to threshold-induced phase transitions fails.

  \item \textbf{Failure of boundary correspondence under mapping.}
  If empirical or simulated enterprise trajectories, when consistently mapped
  to ETS variables, do not exhibit boundary-like changes in phase membership
  at predicate sign changes or observability loss, then the diagnostic claim
  of threshold-induced phase reassignment is refuted.

  \item \textbf{Violation of absorbing constraints.}
  If STOP or other ETS no-go invariants admit outgoing phase-level transitions
  or behave as non-absorbing under the ETS transition allowance relation,
  then the formal phase logic asserted here is invalid.
\end{enumerate}

These falsifiability conditions are stated at the level of formal structure.
They do not require any specific empirical dataset, but they admit operational
tests wherever a faithful mapping to ETS variables is available.
