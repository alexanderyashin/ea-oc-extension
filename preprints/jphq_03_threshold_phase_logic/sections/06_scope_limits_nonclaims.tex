\section{Scope limits and non-claims}
\label{sec:limits}

This paper is strictly diagnostic in scope. It introduces no prescriptions,
interventions, controls, governance mechanisms, or optimization objectives.
Phase identification under ETS predicates is analytically separated from any
notion of action, recovery, steering, or improvement.

No continuity of enterprise evolution is assumed or implied. In particular,
the existence of phase predicates or phase boundaries does not entail the
existence of continuous trajectories, reversible paths, or feasible transitions
between phases.

Key performance indicators (KPIs), metrics, or managerial targets are not treated
as primitives. All variables used in this paper are taken directly and exclusively
from OC Core and ETS.K\_EA.v1.1.

The results presented herein are limited to formal statements about
classification, logical partitioning of admissible states, and
constraint-induced changes in phase membership. Any empirical interpretation,
intervention logic, or policy application lies explicitly outside the scope
of this work.
