\section{Scope limits and non-claims}
\label{sec:limits}

This paper is strictly diagnostic in scope.
It introduces no prescriptions, interventions, controls, governance mechanisms,
or optimization objectives.
Phase identification under ETS predicates is analytically separated from any
notion of action, recovery, steering, or improvement.

No continuity of enterprise evolution is assumed or implied.
In particular, the existence of phase predicates, phase boundaries, or an
allowance relation does not entail the existence of continuous trajectories,
reversible paths, feasible transitions, or controllable dynamics between phases.

Key performance indicators (KPIs), managerial metrics, targets, or benchmarks
are not treated as primitives.
All variables, predicates, and relations used in this paper are taken directly
and exclusively from OC Core and \artifact{ETS.K_EA.v1.1}.
No reinterpretation, aggregation, or substitution of ETS variables is performed.

The results presented herein are limited to formal statements about
classification, logical partitioning of admissible states, and
constraint-induced changes in phase membership.
Any empirical interpretation, intervention logic, governance strategy, or
policy application lies explicitly outside the scope of this work.

\paragraph{Non-teleological stance.}
No phase is interpreted as desirable, optimal, stable, or superior.
Phase labels carry no normative meaning and encode no implicit objective,
direction, or notion of progress.

\paragraph{Non-managerial stance.}
The formalism presented is not a management framework, maturity model,
decision-support system, or best-practice catalogue.
Any such reinterpretation constitutes an external overlay not licensed by
the results of this paper.
