\subsection*{Frozen Question (Q4)}

\emph{Are there formal No-Go theorems that rule out enterprise transformation
under given structural constraints, when enterprises are modeled as continua
$\KEA$ within the Ontology of Continua (\OC) and the executable specification
\kw{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}?}

This question is treated as \emph{frozen}. The present preprint neither
broadens, narrows, nor reinterprets its scope. In particular, the term
\emph{transformation} is understood strictly as a \emph{structural} notion,
defined formally in subsequent sections, and is explicitly distinguished from
temporal evolution, incremental change, process dynamics, or rates of change.

\subsection*{Purpose and Output}

The sole purpose of this work is to determine whether there exist classes of
enterprises, modeled as continua $\KEA$, for which \emph{formal impossibility
results} can be established with respect to transformation. The only admissible
outputs are negative statements of the following form:

\begin{quote}
\emph{Under explicitly stated structural constraints expressed in the
\OC/\kw{ETS} formalism, transformation is impossible.}
\end{quote}

No constructive, corrective, or intervention-oriented results are sought or
implied. The contribution of this artifact is exhausted by the identification,
formal statement, and explicit scope delimitation of such No-Go conditions.

\subsection*{Explicit Non-Goals}

This preprint contains no prescriptions, governance frameworks, optimization
objectives, or control logic. It introduces no managerial narratives, maturity
models, KPIs, benchmarks, or change-management methodologies. It makes no
claims about what enterprises \emph{should} do, nor about how transformation
could be achieved.

The artifact is intentionally stand-alone and purely diagnostic in nature. Its
claims are fully exhausted by formally stated No-Go theorems, their internal
proof obligations within \OC/\kw{ETS}, and the explicitly delimited structural
conditions under which those impossibility results hold.
