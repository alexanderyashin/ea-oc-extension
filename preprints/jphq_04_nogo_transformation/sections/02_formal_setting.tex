We take as immutable input the Ontology of Continua (\OC) Core primitives and
operators together with the executable-closed enterprise continuum
specification \kw{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}. The formal system is treated as given and
internally coherent for the purposes of this preprint. No new primitives,
operators, metrics, mappings, or semantic reinterpretations are introduced.

\paragraph{Primitive vocabulary (assumed).}
An enterprise continuum $\KEA$ is specified by its admissible state set
$\Om(\KEA)$, boundary $\dOm(\KEA)$, axes $\Ax(\KEA)$, potentials $\Pot(\KEA)$,
thresholds $\Th(\KEA)$, flows $\Flow(\KEA)$, cycles $\Cyc(\KEA)$, and continuity
measure $\kcont(\KEA)$. These objects are taken exactly as defined in \OC\ Core
and instantiated for enterprises by \kw{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}. Throughout this
preprint, all references to structure, constraint, admissibility, feasibility,
or non-existence are reducible to this fixed primitive vocabulary.

Diagnostic phase labels $\PHOK$, $\PHIN$, $\PHCO$, and $\PHST$ are admissible as
outcomes of the phase logic already defined in \OC/\kw{ETS}. They are used
strictly as formal classifications of continuum states and transitions, without
any evaluative, normative, teleological, managerial, or interpretive
connotation.

\paragraph{Standing constraint (internal derivability).}
All claims and results in this preprint are conditional on the correctness and
internal coherence of the \OC/\kw{ETS} definitions. Proofs therefore take the
form of internal derivations within that formal system. No empirical premises,
behavioral assumptions, organizational heuristics, statistical regularities, or
external semantic layers are invoked at any point.

\paragraph{Scope of formal reasoning.}
The analysis is confined to structural properties of $\KEA$ as a continuum.
Temporal aspects enter only insofar as they are already encoded in the
admissible flows $\Flow(\KEA)$, cycles $\Cyc(\KEA)$, and phase logic of
\OC/\kw{ETS}. Any statement of impossibility must therefore be attributable
exclusively to structural incompatibilities within $\Om(\KEA)$, $\Ax(\KEA)$,
and $\Th(\KEA)$, or to admissibility constraints imposed by $\dOm(\KEA)$ and
$\Cyc(\KEA)$, rather than to contingent historical, environmental,
organizational, or contextual factors.
