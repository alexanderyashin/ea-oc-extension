\begin{abstract}
We address a single frozen scientific question:
Is \emph{structural diagnosability} a formally well-defined and decidable property
of enterprises modeled as continua (\KEA), independent of managerial intent and
KPI observability?

This preprint provides a formal definition of structural diagnosability strictly
within the immutable \OC{} core and the executable-closed enterprise continuum
specification (ETS.K\_EA.v1.1). We then reduce diagnosability to a finite
decision problem over the enterprise continuum representation, yielding a
decidability result under explicitly stated minimal assumptions already present
in the specification.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Keywords.}
