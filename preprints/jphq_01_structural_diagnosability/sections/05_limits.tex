\subsection{What is not claimed}
We do not claim:
\begin{itemize}
  \item that diagnosability implies controllability, success, or improvement;
  \item any managerial prescription or actionability;
  \item any empirical KPI-based observability guarantee;
  \item any universality beyond the scope of \OC{} and \ETSK.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What would falsify the main claims}
The main claims would be falsified if:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the formal definition of \(D(\KEA)\) cannot be expressed purely in the
        immutable structures provided by \OC{} and \ETSK; or
  \item the ETS representation necessarily requires infinite objects that are not
        reducible to finite decision procedures in the intended encoding.
\end{itemize}
