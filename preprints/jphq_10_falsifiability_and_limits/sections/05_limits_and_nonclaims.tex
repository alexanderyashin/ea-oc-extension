\section{Limits and explicit non-claims}

This section delineates the formal limits of the enterprise-as-continuum
model and states explicit non-claims.
These limits are intrinsic to the Ontology of Continua (OC Core) and the
executable specification ETS.K\_EA.
They follow necessarily from the structure of the formalism and do not
constitute weaknesses, failures, or empirical shortcomings of the model.

\medskip

\begin{nonclaim}[No empirical falsification]
The enterprise-as-continuum model does not admit empirical falsification in
the classical Popperian sense.
Disagreement with empirical data, measurements, case descriptions, or
organizational narratives does not, by itself, constitute falsification.
Only admissible observations that are expressible within the formal
observation language of ETS.K\_EA and that imply violations of mandatory
internal constraints can give rise to falsification.
\end{nonclaim}

\medskip

\begin{nonclaim}[No falsification by non-identifiability]
The inability to uniquely reconstruct a model realization from admissible
observations does not falsify the model.
Underdetermination, ambiguity, partial observability, and the coexistence of
multiple compatible realizations in \(\Omega(K_{EA})\) are formally
admissible outcomes.
Such situations reflect limits of identifiability, not internal
inconsistency.
\end{nonclaim}

\medskip

\begin{nonclaim}[No falsification by undecidability]
Logical undecidability of a query over the enterprise state space
\(\Omega(K_{EA})\) does not imply falsification.
Undecidability reflects limits of inference, computation, or expressive
power within the formal system, not violations of axioms, invariants,
thresholds, or structural constraints.
\end{nonclaim}

\medskip

\begin{nonclaim}[STOP is not model failure]
A \emph{STOP} outcome, defined as the formal impossibility of continuing
diagnosis without violating mandatory model constraints, is a boundary
condition of applicability.
It indicates exhaustion of admissible inference paths under the given
observation interface and is explicitly classified as a non-falsifying
result.
STOP does not assert the non-existence of admissible model realizations.
\end{nonclaim}

\medskip

\begin{nonclaim}[No claims beyond the OC/ETS domain]
The enterprise-as-continuum model makes no claims about phenomena, entities,
relations, or causal mechanisms that are not representable within the formal
vocabulary of OC Core and ETS.K\_EA.
Statements formulated outside this domain are neither supported, predicted,
explained, nor contradicted by the model.
They are formally out of scope.
\end{nonclaim}

\medskip

\begin{remark}
Taken together, these limits define a sharp boundary between falsification,
non-applicability, and epistemic limitation.
Falsification occurs exclusively through internal inconsistency with the
formal state space \(\Omega(K_{EA})\).
All other apparent failure modes are excluded by construction and constitute
well-defined, non-falsifying outcomes of the formalism.
\end{remark}
