\section{Problem Statement}

Let the enterprise be modeled as a continuum $K_{EA}$ with admissible state space
$\Omega(K_{EA})$ and threshold system $\Theta$.
An \emph{intervention} is any operator that maps states to states under the
constraints of $\Theta$.

The question is intentionally negative:

\begin{quote}
\emph{Where is intervention impossible, self-defeating, or structurally
destructive, independent of intent, competence, or declared objectives?}
\end{quote}

We do not ask for optimal intervention; we ask for \emph{structural} limits
implied by the existence of boundaries $\partial\Omega$ and non-negotiable
threshold constraints $\Theta$.
