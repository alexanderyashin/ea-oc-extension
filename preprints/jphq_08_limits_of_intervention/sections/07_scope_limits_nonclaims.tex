\section{Scope Limits and Non-Claims}

\paragraph{Scope.}
We analyze only the \emph{structural feasibility limits} of interventions treated
as abstract operators on $\Omega$ under $\ThetaSys$.

\paragraph{Non-claims.}
This work does \emph{not}:
\begin{itemize}
\item propose interventions, governance methods, recovery mechanisms, or
optimization strategies;
\item provide empirical case studies or managerial prescriptions;
\item argue about desirability, ethics, or policy.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Interpretation discipline.}
Any ``actionable'' reading is out of scope. The results should be read as
no-go constraints: conditions under which intervention must incur either
continuity loss or phase change.
