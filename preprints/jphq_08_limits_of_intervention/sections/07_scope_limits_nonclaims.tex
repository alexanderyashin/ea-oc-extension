\section{Scope Limits and Non-Claims}

\paragraph{Scope.}
This work analyzes only the \emph{structural feasibility limits} of interventions
treated as abstract operators on the admissible state space $\Omega$ under the
threshold system $\ThetaSys$.
The analysis is static and structural: it concerns what mappings are possible
under admissibility constraints, not how interventions are planned, executed, or
governed in practice.

\paragraph{Non-claims.}
Accordingly, this work does \emph{not}:
\begin{itemize}
\item propose concrete interventions, governance frameworks, recovery strategies,
or optimization methods;
\item provide empirical case studies, simulations, or organizational prescriptions;
\item make claims about desirability, ethics, policy, or managerial best practice;
\item address probabilistic success, expected outcomes, or cost--benefit tradeoffs.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Interpretation discipline.}
The results must be read strictly as \emph{no-go constraints}.
They identify conditions under which intervention is structurally forced to incur
either continuity loss or a phase change.
Any attempt to interpret these results as actionable guidance, performance advice,
or operational recommendation lies outside the scope of this work.
