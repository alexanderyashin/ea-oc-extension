\section{Formal Setting}

We assume OC Core as the foundational formalism~\cite{oc_core}.
We further adopt the executable-closed ETS.K\_EA.v1.1 as an immutable symbol
discipline and constraint set for the enterprise continuum~\cite{ets_kea_v11}.
No new axioms are introduced.

\subsection{Enterprise continuum and admissibility}

We write the enterprise continuum as
\[
K_{EA} \equiv (\Omega,\partial\Omega, A, P, \ThetaSys, J, C, k, \tau),
\]
where $\Omega=\Omega(K_{EA})$ is the admissible state space and $\partial\Omega$
is its boundary. The remaining components are the OC primitives: axes $A$,
potentials $P$, threshold system $\ThetaSys$, flows $J$, closure cycles $C$,
continuity measure $k$, and characteristic cycle times $\tau$ (all in the OC
Core sense).

Thresholds are treated as a \emph{system} (possibly vector-valued) of
constraints on states:
\[
\ThetaSys(\omega) \le 0
\quad \text{means: ``$\omega$ is admissible under all thresholds.''}
\]
Define the admissible region
\[
\Omega \;:=\; \{\omega \in \mathcal{S} \mid \ThetaSys(\omega)\le 0\},
\]
for some ambient space $\mathcal{S}$ of candidate states. Then $\partial\Omega$
denotes the boundary between admissible and inadmissible states.

\subsection{Continuity and phase labels}

The continuity measure is a map
\[
k:\Omega \to [0,1],
\]
interpreted as structural continuity/connectedness of the enterprise continuum
(as defined by OC Core/ETS).

We use a phase classifier
\[
\Phase:\Omega \to \{\OK,\INERTIA,\COLLAPSE,\STOP\},
\]
treated as a coarse label determined by OC/ETS-consistent criteria (e.g.,
closure loss, threshold death, cycle divergence). We do not fix an explicit
classifier; the results depend only on the existence of a phase partition and
the terminal nature of $\STOP$.

\begin{remark}
The role of $\partial\Omega$ is not ``small perturbations'' but structural
non-smoothness: near $\partial\Omega$ the admissible set changes discontinuously
in what mappings are allowed, because admissibility is governed by inequality
constraints.
\end{remark}
