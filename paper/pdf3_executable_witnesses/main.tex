\begin{abstract}
PDF-3 is a closure and registry artifact for diagnostic witness labels used across the
frozen KEA artifact chain (PDF-0/1/2). It introduces no new semantics, predicates,
metrics, thresholds, or execution logic. Its sole function is structural: (i) to anchor
a minimal witness label set to inherited phase classifications, (ii) to provide explicit
dependency closure to upstream immutable artifacts, and (iii) to state refutation
conditions for witness registration under inherited semantics (including STOP as a
terminal epistemic boundary).
