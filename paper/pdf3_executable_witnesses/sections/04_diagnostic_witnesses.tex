\section{Diagnostic Witnesses (Non-Executable)}

This section defines a minimal and sufficient set of \emph{diagnostic witnesses}
for PDF-3. Each witness is specified at a purely formal, non-executable level.

\textbf{Scope constraints (hard).}
All semantics are inherited \emph{without modification} from the frozen artifacts:
PDF-0 (KEA Whitepaper), PDF-1 (Executable Demonstration Artifact), and
PDF-2 (Structural Diagnosability \& Phase Logic). No new predicates, thresholds,
metrics, or diagrams are introduced.

\textbf{Epistemic stance.}
All witnesses are \textbf{diagnostic-only}, \textbf{non-optimizing}, and
\textbf{non-prescriptive}. The witnesses do not imply control, remediation,
intervention, or optimization.

\subsection{Witness Set Overview}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|p{8.2cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Witness} & \textbf{Target Class} & \textbf{Diagnostic Role} \\
\hline
W\textsubscript{S} & SUCCESS &
Demonstrates sustained structural viability under the already-defined phase logic. \\
\hline
W\textsubscript{I} & INERTIA &
Demonstrates bounded persistence without structural progression, while remaining diagnosable. \\
\hline
W\textsubscript{C} & COLLAPSE &
Demonstrates irreversible loss of structural viability as classified by the inherited semantics. \\
\hline
W\textsubscript{STOP} & STOP &
Demonstrates terminal epistemic non-diagnosability (diagnostic closure by STOP). \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Witness W\_S: SUCCESS}

\textbf{Purpose (diagnostic role).}
Witness that the evaluated configuration is classified as SUCCESS under the inherited semantics.

\textbf{Inputs (conceptual; non-executable).}
A phase-space configuration admissible under the definitions and constraints of PDF-1,
evaluated via the phase logic of PDF-2.

\textbf{Expected diagnostic classification.}
SUCCESS.

\textbf{Termination condition.}
Terminates once SUCCESS classification is obtained under continued diagnosability
(i.e., without triggering STOP).

\subsection{Witness W\_I: INERTIA}

\textbf{Purpose (diagnostic role).}
Witness that the evaluated configuration is classified as INERTIA: persistent, bounded,
and diagnosable, yet non-progressive under the inherited semantics.

\textbf{Inputs (conceptual; non-executable).}
A phase-space configuration remaining within admissible bounds as defined in PDF-1/2,
while the inherited classification yields INERTIA.

\textbf{Expected diagnostic classification.}
INERTIA.

\textbf{Termination condition.}
Terminates upon transition (under the inherited phase logic) to SUCCESS, COLLAPSE, or STOP.

\subsection{Witness W\_C: COLLAPSE}

\textbf{Purpose (diagnostic role).}
Witness that the evaluated configuration is classified as COLLAPSE under the inherited semantics.

\textbf{Inputs (conceptual; non-executable).}
A phase-space configuration that, when evaluated using the frozen PDF-1/2 semantics,
is classified as COLLAPSE.

\textbf{Expected diagnostic classification.}
COLLAPSE.

\textbf{Termination condition.}
Terminates immediately upon COLLAPSE classification.

\subsection{Witness W\_{STOP}: STOP}

\textbf{Purpose (diagnostic role).}
Witness that evaluation terminates epistemically via STOP due to loss of diagnosability.

\textbf{Inputs (conceptual; non-executable).}
A phase-space configuration for which the inherited diagnosability conditions
from PDF-1/2 are not met, yielding STOP.

\textbf{Expected diagnostic classification.}
STOP.

\textbf{Termination condition.}
Immediate termination upon STOP.

\textbf{STOP clause (mandatory).}
STOP is treated as a \textbf{terminal epistemic outcome}, not as a failure,
error, or defect. STOP does not imply remediation or intervention.

\subsection{Non-Prescription Clause}

These witness definitions do not prescribe action and do not optimize any objective.
They only define how diagnostic closure is witnessed under the frozen semantics.
Any prescriptive use is out of scope for PDF-3.
