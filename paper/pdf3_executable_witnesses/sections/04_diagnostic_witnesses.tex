\section{Diagnostic Witnesses (Non-Executable)}

\paragraph{Role of Witnesses in PDF-3.}
In PDF-3, a \emph{diagnostic witness} is neither an execution trace,
nor an algorithm, proof, or empirical observation.
It is a \emph{structural closure token} that certifies the outcome
of an evaluation performed entirely in upstream artifacts.

Witnesses exist to make diagnostic outcomes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item referentially explicit,
  \item unambiguous across documents,
  \item stable under frozen dependencies.
\end{itemize}

They provide a formally fixed interface between executable diagnostics
and downstream interpretation, without reintroducing execution or semantics.

\paragraph{Non-Executability by Construction.}
All witnesses defined in this section are intentionally non-executable.
This is not an omission but a structural necessity imposed by the artifact hierarchy.

\begin{quote}
Evaluation, execution, and classification occur exclusively in upstream artifacts.
PDF-3 records only the diagnostic closure of those evaluations.
\end{quote}

Any executable content at this level would duplicate or override inherited semantics,
violating dependency closure and invalidating the artifact class.

\paragraph{Hard Semantic Constraint.}
All semantics used to justify witness registration are inherited
\emph{verbatim and without modification} from the frozen upstream artifacts:
PDF-0 (KEA Whitepaper), PDF-1 (Executable Demonstration Artifact),
and PDF-2 (Structural Diagnosability \& Phase Logic).

PDF-3 introduces no predicates, thresholds, metrics, evaluative procedures,
or formal conditions of its own.

\paragraph{Epistemic Discipline.}
All witnesses are strictly:
\begin{itemize}
  \item diagnostic-only,
  \item non-optimizing,
  \item non-prescriptive.
\end{itemize}

No witness implies control, remediation, intervention, optimization,
or normative judgment beyond inherited diagnostic classification.

\subsection{Canonical Witness Set (Closure Completeness)}

PDF-3 fixes a minimal, finite, and sufficient canonical set
of diagnostic witness labels.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|p{8.6cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Witness} & \textbf{Target Class} & \textbf{Structural Diagnostic Meaning} \\
\hline
$W_{\mathrm{S}}$ & SUCCESS &
Inherited evaluation yields SUCCESS under preserved diagnosability. \\
\hline
$W_{\mathrm{I}}$ & INERTIA &
Inherited evaluation yields INERTIA:
bounded, persistent diagnosability without structural progression. \\
\hline
$W_{\mathrm{C}}$ & COLLAPSE &
Inherited evaluation yields COLLAPSE:
irreversible loss of structural viability. \\
\hline
$W_{\mathrm{STOP}}$ & STOP &
Inherited diagnosability conditions fail;
evaluation terminates without classification. \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Closure Completeness Statement.}
This witness set is \emph{exhaustive} with respect to inherited semantics:

\begin{quote}
For every admissible evaluation outcome under the frozen upstream semantics,
there exists a witness label in this set that is registrable in PDF-3.
\end{quote}

No additional witness category can be introduced
without modifying upstream diagnostic logic.

\subsection{Witness Registration Principle}

A witness label $W_X$ is registrable in PDF-3 if and only if:

\begin{quote}
An evaluation performed under frozen upstream semantics
(PDF-1 and PDF-2) yields the diagnostic class $X$,
and no violation of diagnosability invalidates that evaluation.
\end{quote}

PDF-3 neither defines nor executes this evaluation.
It records only the resulting closure label.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Witness $W_{\mathrm{S}}$: SUCCESS}{Witness WS: SUCCESS}}

\textbf{Diagnostic role.}
Registers that a referenced configuration is classified as SUCCESS
under inherited semantics.

\textbf{Conceptual input (non-executable).}
A phase-space configuration admissible under the frozen constraints of PDF-1,
evaluated using the phase logic defined in PDF-2.

\textbf{Closure condition.}
The inherited classifier yields SUCCESS
and diagnosability is preserved.

\textbf{Refutation (inherited).}
$W_{\mathrm{S}}$ is invalid if:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the inherited classifier does not yield SUCCESS, or
  \item diagnosability is lost and STOP is triggered.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Witness $W_{\mathrm{I}}$: INERTIA}{Witness WI: INERTIA}}

\textbf{Diagnostic role.}
Registers that a referenced configuration is classified as INERTIA
under inherited semantics.

\textbf{Conceptual input (non-executable).}
A phase-space configuration remaining admissible under frozen constraints
while yielding the INERTIA classification.

\textbf{Closure condition.}
The inherited classifier yields INERTIA
without loss of diagnosability.

\textbf{Refutation (inherited).}
$W_{\mathrm{I}}$ is invalid if:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the classifier yields SUCCESS, COLLAPSE, or STOP, or
  \item admissibility conditions are violated.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Witness $W_{\mathrm{C}}$: COLLAPSE}{Witness WC: COLLAPSE}}

\textbf{Diagnostic role.}
Registers irreversible loss of structural viability
classified as COLLAPSE under inherited semantics.

\textbf{Conceptual input (non-executable).}
A configuration that, when evaluated under frozen upstream semantics,
yields COLLAPSE.

\textbf{Closure condition.}
Immediate classification as COLLAPSE.

\textbf{Refutation (inherited).}
$W_{\mathrm{C}}$ is invalid if the inherited classifier
does not yield COLLAPSE.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Witness $W_{\mathrm{STOP}}$: STOP}{Witness WSTOP: STOP}}

\textbf{Diagnostic role.}
Registers terminal epistemic non-diagnosability.

\textbf{Conceptual input (non-executable).}
A configuration for which inherited diagnosability conditions
are not satisfied.

\textbf{Closure condition.}
Immediate termination of evaluation via STOP.

\textbf{STOP Axiom (Terminality).}
STOP is a \emph{terminal epistemic boundary condition}.
It is not a failure, error, defect, or system state.
No diagnostic, evaluative, inferential, or prescriptive
operation is permitted beyond STOP.

\textbf{Refutation (inherited).}
$W_{\mathrm{STOP}}$ is invalid if diagnosability remains satisfied
under frozen upstream semantics.

\subsection{Non-Prescription Invariant}

Witness registration in PDF-3 has no operational consequences.
It neither implies nor authorizes action, intervention,
optimization, or decision-making.

The role of PDF-3 is exhausted by diagnostic closure.
