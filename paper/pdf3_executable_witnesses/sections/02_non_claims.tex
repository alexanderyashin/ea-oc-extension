\section{Non-Claims}

\paragraph{Explicit Non-Claims.}
PDF-3 explicitly makes no claims of any kind regarding:
\begin{itemize}
  \item correctness, completeness, sufficiency, robustness, or optimality,
  \item applicability to any specific organization, system, domain, or context,
  \item predictive, prescriptive, normative, advisory, or optimization outcomes,
  \item empirical validation, performance characteristics, or effectiveness,
  \item success, failure, or quality of any execution.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Structural Nature of Non-Claims.}
The absence of such claims is not incidental or defensive.
It is a \emph{structural property} of the artifact class instantiated by PDF-3.

PDF-3 does not evaluate systems, executions, models, organizations, or decisions.
It neither asserts nor implies desirable outcomes, recommended actions, or
performance expectations.
Its function is strictly limited to the formal closure and registration of
diagnostic classifications inherited from upstream artifacts.

\paragraph{Interpretation Boundary.}
Any interpretation of this document as:
\begin{itemize}
  \item an evaluation of a system,
  \item an assessment of organizational capability,
  \item a recommendation, guideline, or method,
  \item or an operational or managerial instrument
\end{itemize}
constitutes a \emph{category error} relative to the declared artifact class.

Such interpretations are invalid independently of whether they appear
plausible, useful, or aligned with external goals.

\paragraph{Separation of Concerns.}
All claims concerning empirical behavior, operational effectiveness,
organizational outcomes, or decision quality—if made at all—belong exclusively
to contexts external to PDF-3.
They are neither supported, implied, nor contradicted by this document.

PDF-3 terminates diagnostic meaning; it does not extend it.
