\section{Artifact Class}

This document is classified as an \emph{Executable Witness \& Diagnostic Closure Artifact}.

The term \emph{Executable} refers exclusively to executable bindings and procedures
defined in upstream artifacts (in particular, PDF-1). This document itself contains
no runnable components, implementations, or execution logic.

Its role is to provide a structural container and registry for witness references and
to formally close diagnostic classifications established under inherited semantics
from upstream artifacts.

No new semantics, logic, predicates, metrics, thresholds, or interpretations are
introduced in this artifact. All diagnostic meaning is strictly inherited.
