\section{High-Level Structure}

\paragraph{Structural Role.}
PDF-3 is not organized as a narrative, method description, or executable module.
Its structure follows directly from its declared artifact class:
a diagnostic closure and registry layer positioned above executable and semantic artifacts.

As such, the structure of PDF-3 is intentionally minimal and non-creative.
It exists to make explicit what is already fixed elsewhere.

\paragraph{Constituent Structural Elements.}
The artifact consists exclusively of the following structural components:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a canonical registry of diagnostic witness labels used for closure bookkeeping,
  \item explicit references from each witness label to its originating diagnostic predicates
        and phase classifications defined in upstream artifacts,
  \item closure markers that bind each witness label to a frozen dependency set
        (PDF-0, PDF-1, PDF-2).
\end{itemize}

No additional structural elements are permitted without violating the declared
scope and role of the artifact.

\paragraph{Absence of Execution by Construction.}
No witnesses are implemented, instantiated, or executed within PDF-3.
This absence is not a limitation or omission.
It is a direct consequence of the artifact’s position in the document chain.

All executable bindings, operational procedures, and evaluation logic reside
exclusively in upstream artifacts (in particular PDF-1).
Embedding or duplicating execution logic here would constitute a structural error,
resulting in semantic ambiguity and invalidating dependency closure.

\paragraph{Closure Invariant.}
PDF-3 satisfies the following invariant:

\begin{quote}
Every diagnostic label appearing in this artifact is fully determined by,
and referentially reducible to, frozen upstream semantics.
\end{quote}

The artifact introduces no new axes of variation, interpretation, or execution.
Its structure is exhausted by registry, reference, and closure.
