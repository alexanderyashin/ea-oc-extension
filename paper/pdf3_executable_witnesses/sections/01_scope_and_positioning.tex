\section{Scope and Positioning}

\paragraph{Scope Definition.}
PDF-3 operates exclusively as a \emph{diagnostic closure and registry layer}
within a fixed artifact chain.
Its scope is strictly limited to referencing, labeling, and closing
diagnostic outcomes defined elsewhere.

PDF-3 depends \emph{only and entirely} on a frozen set of upstream artifacts.
It introduces no independent semantics, diagnostics, execution logic,
interpretive authority, or evaluative criteria of its own.

\paragraph{Complete Upstream Dependency Set.}
All content in PDF-3 is defined relative to the following upstream artifacts:
\begin{itemize}
  \item PDF-0: KEA Whitepaper (foundational formal specification),
  \item PDF-1: Executable Demonstration Artifact (executable bindings and evaluation procedures),
  \item PDF-2: Structural Diagnosability \& Phase Logic (diagnostic phase semantics).
\end{itemize}
No other documents, implementations, datasets, or interpretations
are within scope for this artifact.

\paragraph{Non-Extension Guarantee.}
PDF-3 does not modify, reinterpret, extend, refine, operationalize,
or contextualize any content originating in the upstream artifacts.

All semantics, classifications, predicates, thresholds, phase definitions,
and diagnostic meanings are inherited \emph{verbatim and without transformation}.
Any semantic effect observable in PDF-3 is reducible to upstream definitions.

\paragraph{Position Within the Artifact Chain.}
PDF-3 occupies a strictly terminal position in the artifact sequence.
Its role is limited to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item anchoring witness labels to inherited diagnostic classifications,
  \item declaring explicit refutation conditions for those labels,
  \item formally closing the diagnostic classification space defined upstream.
\end{itemize}

PDF-3 performs no inference, evaluation, execution, or validation.
It records diagnostic closure only.

\paragraph{Non-Autonomy Constraint.}
PDF-3 is \emph{intentionally non-autonomous}.
Its validity, meaning, and interpretability are entirely derivative:
\begin{quote}
\emph{In the absence of the frozen upstream artifacts,
PDF-3 has no semantic content, no diagnostic meaning,
and no executable interpretation.}
\end{quote}
This non-autonomy is not a limitation but a defining structural property
of the artifact class.
