\section*{Abstract}

\textbf{OPS-0} defines the \emph{OPS-0 artifact class} as a strictly \textbf{diagnostic-only} and \textbf{scope-locked} \textbf{Operational Semantics \& Tooling Contract} for EA-OC-EXTENSION artefacts.
It specifies \emph{admissible} and \emph{forbidden} tool-visible operations, input admissibility constraints, output legitimacy requirements, and \textbf{STOP / NO-GO} classification as \textbf{terminal epistemic boundaries}.

OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} introduce new Ontology of Continua (OC) primitives and \textbf{MUST NOT} reinterpret, extend, or modify the semantics of \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}.
OPS-0 \textbf{MUST} remain a contract for \emph{classification, validation, and terminal outcome emission} only; it \textbf{MUST NOT} provide recommendations, remediation logic, optimization objectives, control prescriptions, execution guidance, or recovery semantics.

Normative requirements in OPS-0 are expressed \textbf{exclusively} using the keywords \textbf{MUST}, \textbf{MUST NOT}, and \textbf{MAY}.
All other language is non-normative and carries no contractual force.

The OPS-0 enforcement surface is limited to \emph{classification outcomes and explicit artefact emission} (including STOP).
It explicitly excludes execution behavior, control flow, implementation detail, or operational governance.

All normative registries, precedence rules, and admissibility classifications are defined \textbf{exclusively} in the Appendix contract tables (CT-xx), which constitute the sole authoritative registry surface.
Main sections may reference these registries but \textbf{MUST NOT} define, populate, or operationalize them.

In all cases, \textbf{STOP is terminal}.
Any activity occurring after STOP is \textbf{outside} the OPS-0 contract.
Continuation beyond STOP requires an external, out-of-band witness and a new run; such a witness has no normative authority within OPS-0 and does not retroactively affect classification outcomes.
