\section*{Abstract}
\textbf{OPS-0} defines the \emph{OPS-0 artifact class} as an \textbf{Operational Semantics \& Tooling Contract} for EA-OC-EXTENSION artifacts.
It is \textbf{scope-locked} and \textbf{diagnostic-only}: it specifies \emph{admissible} and \emph{forbidden} tool-visible operations, input admissibility rules, output legitimacy requirements, and \textbf{STOP / NO-GO enforcement} as terminal epistemic boundaries.

OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} introduce new Ontology of Continua (OC) primitives and \textbf{MUST NOT} reinterpret or modify the semantics of ETS.K\_EA.v1.1.
OPS-0 \textbf{MUST} remain a contract for \emph{classification, validation, and enforcement} only; it \textbf{MUST NOT} provide recommendations, remediation steps, optimization objectives, or control prescriptions.

Normative requirements in OPS-0 use \textbf{MUST / MUST NOT / MAY}.
The enforcement surface, registries, and precedence rules are defined in the main sections and bound to Appendix registries (CT-xx tables) referenced there.
In all cases, \textbf{STOP is terminal}: any post-STOP activity is \textbf{outside} this contract and requires an external witness and a new run, not a continuation within OPS-0.
