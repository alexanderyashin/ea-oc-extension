\section{Operational Primitives (Declared Classification Surfaces)}

\subsection{Non-Introduction Rule (Binding)}
RUN-OPS0-01 \textbf{MUST} introduce \textbf{no new Ontology of Continua (OC) primitives}
and \textbf{MUST NOT} reinterpret, overload, extend, substitute,
or otherwise alter the semantics of \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}.

Any term, symbol, label, or concept named in this section
\textbf{DOES NOT} constitute an operational, computational,
procedural, inferential, or executable primitive.

All named elements are \textbf{classification surfaces only}.
They exist solely to anchor contract language,
section structure, and appendix registry bindings,
and carry \textbf{no executable, evaluative,
inferential, or decision-producing semantics}
within OPS-0.

\subsection{Declared Classification Surfaces (Non-Executable)}
The following domains are declared as
\textbf{normative classification surfaces only}.

Their sole purpose is to support:
\begin{itemize}
  \item contract completeness and closure,
  \item unambiguous internal and external referencing,
  \item exclusive binding to appendix registries (CT-xx),
  \item and auditability of STOP / NO-GO classifications.
\end{itemize}

Within RUN-OPS0-01, these domains
\textbf{MUST NOT} be interpreted as actions,
operations, procedures, evaluations,
algorithms, workflows, or tool capabilities.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Artifact identifiers and version labels}:
        declared exclusively as reference anchors.
        They define no structure, format,
        validation logic, comparison rule,
        or processing semantics.
  \item \textbf{Named operational labels}
        (e.g.\ Op-$\Omega$, Op-A, Op-$\Theta$):
        declared as \emph{classification labels only}.
        They denote registry-bound diagnostic categories
        and \textbf{MUST NOT} be interpreted
        as executable operations, functions,
        tool actions, or callable behaviors.
  \item \textbf{STOP / NO-GO classification surface}:
        STOP denotes a terminal epistemic classification.
        Its presence represents a \emph{classification outcome only},
        not a process step, control signal,
        execution directive, or behavioral instruction.
  \item \textbf{Provenance and authorship surface}:
        mandatory as a contract-level requirement,
        but without any defined structure, schema,
        serialization, verification, enforcement,
        or implementation mechanism in this run.
\end{itemize}

Any interpretation of the above domains
as implying tool behavior, execution order,
evaluation logic, procedural flow,
or operational capability
\textbf{constitutes a contract violation}
under OPS-0.

\subsection{Strict Reservation and Non-Effect Clause}
All semantic population, operational interpretation,
procedural meaning, admissibility logic,
detection rules, predicates, metrics,
schemas, formats, tests, examples,
or executable logic associated with the
declared classification surfaces above
are \textbf{explicitly reserved}
for RUN-OPS0-02 and subsequent runs.

Such future content:
\begin{itemize}
  \item has \textbf{no normative, interpretive,
        or inferential effect} on RUN-OPS0-01;
  \item \textbf{MUST NOT} be retroactively inferred,
        assumed, or projected onto this run;
  \item \textbf{MUST} be explicitly versioned,
        scope-declared, and contractually bound
        when introduced.
\end{itemize}
