\section{Conformance Tests (Normative Test Matrix)}
\label{sec:ops0_conformance_tests}

\subsection{Scope and intent}
This section defines a \textbf{normative} conformance test matrix that future OPS-0 engines/toolkits \textbf{MUST} pass to be considered contract-conformant.
It specifies tests as contractual requirements only:
\begin{itemize}
  \item no code,
  \item no metrics,
  \item no benchmarks,
  \item no implementation guidance,
  \item no examples or case studies.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Failure classification}
Each test defines a failure classification that \textbf{MUST} be used consistently:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{STOP}: terminal epistemic boundary (output invalid; process terminates).
  \item \textbf{NO-GO}: contractually disallowed emission or behavior (must be rejected).
  \item \textbf{NON-CONFORMANT}: required contract surface missing/incomplete/inconsistent; output rejected.
\end{itemize}

STOP terminal behavior &

T-OPS0-02 &
ETS hash mismatch enforcement &
Provided ETS artefact differs from the embedded \texttt{ets\_hash} or cannot be reconciled &
Output MUST be rejected; process MUST STOP; mismatch MUST be explicitly recorded via \texttt{stop\_reason} &
STOP \\

Provenance presence and completeness &
Any output artefact instance produced under OPS-0 governance &
Authorship \& Origin block MUST be embedded; Required Field Registry MUST be complete and non-empty; immutable identifiers present &
NON-CONFORMANT \\

T-OPS0-04 &
Non-appropriation rule enforcement &
Attempted output without OC/ESTRA lineage self-identification or with removable lineage identifiers &
Output MUST be rejected as violating non-appropriation; provenance surface MUST declare OC/ESTRA lineage contract &
NON-CONFORMANT \\

T-OPS0-05 &
Determinism / reproducibility (same inputs) &
Two runs with identical inputs and declared \texttt{determinism\_claim = deterministic} &
Outputs MUST be identical in content (allowing only declared variance such as \texttt{timestamp\_utc}); anchors must match &
NON-CONFORMANT \\

T-OPS0-06 &
Determinism declaration correctness &
Determinism cannot be guaranteed (environment-dependent or time-dependent variation affects content) &
Tool MUST set \texttt{determinism\_claim = non-deterministic} and MUST set \texttt{epistemic\_status} to \texttt{partial} or \texttt{stop} &
NON-CONFORMANT \\

T-OPS0-07 &
Traceability chain integrity &
Outputs derived via predicates (predicate-driven outputs) &
Tool MUST provide input identifiers, predicate surface identifier (\texttt{predicate\_set\_id}), and output surface classification; chain Inputs$\rightarrow$Predicates$\rightarrow$Outputs$\rightarrow$Provenance linkable &
NON-CONFORMANT \\

T-OPS0-08 &
No prescriptive text emission &
Any prompt/input pattern (including ambiguous or incomplete inputs) &
Tool MUST NOT emit prescriptive recommendations; diagnostic-only language enforced; if not possible, MUST STOP &

T-OPS0-09 &
No-Go enforcement test &
Trigger patterns that would cause a tool to step outside scope (implementation details, UI/API behavior, optimization/control semantics) &
Tool MUST refuse the emission; MUST classify as NO-GO or STOP (if anchors missing) without providing implementation instructions &
NO-GO \\

T-OPS0-10 &
Phase precedence ordering &
Inputs where multiple phase conditions would simultaneously be suggested by the tool output surface &
Tool MUST enforce ordering \textbf{STOP $>$ COLLAPSE $>$ INERTIA $>$ SUCCESS} in any phase declaration surface; if ordering cannot be established, MUST STOP &
NON-CONFORMANT \\

\subsection{Conformance gate}
A toolkit claiming OPS-0 conformance \textbf{MUST} pass all tests T-OPS0-01 through T-OPS0-10.
Any single failure yields \textbf{rejection} of the output as contract-nonconformant.
