\section{Purpose and Scope (Locked)}

\subsection{Purpose}
OPS-0 defines a \textbf{contract scaffold} that \textbf{MUST} constrain the observable behavior of tools and the admissibility of tool-visible transformations applied to EA-OC-EXTENSION artifacts.
The contract is \textbf{diagnostic-only}: it classifies, validates, and enforces admissibility boundaries without prescribing actions or outcomes.

OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} introduce new Ontology of Continua (OC) primitives and \textbf{MUST NOT} reinterpret, extend, or modify the semantics of ETS.K\_EA.v1.1.
OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} provide recommendations, remediation guidance, optimization objectives, or control strategies.

\subsection{Scope Lock}
This RUN-OPS0-01 deliverable \textbf{MUST} provide only:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the locked artifact class definition (\emph{Operational Semantics \& Tooling Contract});
  \item explicit diagnostic-only and non-claim boundary conditions;
  \item a complete section map with normative bindings to appendix registries (CT-xx tables), without populating those registries;
  \item a lineage statement to PDF-0, PDF-2, and PDF-3 establishing separation of concerns.
\end{itemize}

Any content not listed above \textbf{MUST NOT} be considered part of OPS-0 and is \textbf{explicitly out of scope} for this run.

\subsection{Reserved}
All substantive content population, including registry entries, concrete formats, mechanisms, and examples, \textbf{MUST} be deferred to RUN-OPS0-02 and subsequent runs.
