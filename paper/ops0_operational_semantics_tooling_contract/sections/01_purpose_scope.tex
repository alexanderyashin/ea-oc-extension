\section{Purpose and Scope (Locked)}

\subsection{Purpose}
OPS-0 defines a \textbf{contract scaffold} that \textbf{MUST} constrain the \emph{observable classification outcomes} of tools and the admissibility of tool-visible transformations applied to EA-OC-EXTENSION artefacts.
Within this contract, \textbf{enforcement} denotes \emph{classification and terminal outcome emission only} (including STOP) and explicitly excludes execution control, remediation, optimization, governance, or behavioral steering.

The contract is \textbf{diagnostic-only}: it classifies and validates admissibility boundaries and emits explicit terminal outcomes, without prescribing actions, outcomes, implementations, procedures, or operational mechanisms.

OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} introduce new Ontology of Continua (OC) primitives and \textbf{MUST NOT} reinterpret, extend, or modify the semantics of \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}.
OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} provide recommendations, remediation guidance, optimization objectives, control strategies, recovery logic, or execution semantics of any kind.

\subsection{Scope Lock}
This RUN-OPS0-01 deliverable \textbf{MUST} provide \emph{only} the following contract-level elements:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the locked artifact class definition (\emph{Operational Semantics \& Tooling Contract});
  \item explicit diagnostic-only, non-prescriptive, and non-claim boundary conditions;
  \item a complete section map with \emph{normative references} to appendix registries (CT-xx tables), without defining, populating, or operationalizing registry content;
  \item a lineage statement to PDF-0, PDF-2, and PDF-3 establishing separation of concerns and provenance context only.
\end{itemize}

No section of OPS-0 may introduce obligations, semantics, detection logic, thresholds, or requirements beyond these elements.
Any content not listed above \textbf{MUST NOT} be considered part of OPS-0 and is \textbf{explicitly out of scope} for this run.

\subsection{Run Finality and Reservation}
RUN-OPS0-01 constitutes a \textbf{closed, scope-locked, and self-contained contract artefact}.
The absence of populated registries, concrete formats, mechanisms, schemas, detection rules, examples, or implementations is \textbf{intentional} and \textbf{MUST NOT} be interpreted as incompleteness, provisional status, or deferred validity.

All substantive content population, including registry entries, concrete formats, mechanisms, schemas, examples, and tests, \textbf{MUST} be deferred to RUN-OPS0-02 and subsequent runs and has no normative effect on RUN-OPS0-01.
