\section{Purpose and Scope (Locked)}

\subsection{Purpose}
OPS-0 defines a \textbf{contract scaffold} that \textbf{MUST} constrain the
\emph{observable classification outcomes} of tools and the admissibility of
tool-visible transformations applied to EA-OC-EXTENSION artefacts.

Within this contract, \textbf{enforcement} denotes \emph{classification and
terminal outcome emission only} (including STOP) and explicitly excludes
execution control, remediation, optimization, governance, orchestration,
or behavioral steering of any kind.

The contract is \textbf{diagnostic-only}: it classifies admissibility boundaries
and validates contract conformance by emitting explicit terminal outcomes,
without prescribing actions, results, implementations, procedures,
pipelines, or operational mechanisms.

OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} introduce new Ontology of Continua (OC) primitives and
\textbf{MUST NOT} reinterpret, extend, override, or modify the semantics of
\texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}.

OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} provide recommendations, remediation guidance,
optimization objectives, control strategies, recovery logic,
or execution semantics of any kind, whether explicit or implied.

\subsection{Scope Lock}
This RUN-OPS0-01 deliverable \textbf{MUST} provide \emph{only} the following
contract-level elements:

\begin{itemize}
  \item the locked artifact class definition
        (\emph{Operational Semantics \& Tooling Contract});
  \item explicit diagnostic-only, non-prescriptive,
        and non-claim boundary conditions;
  \item a complete section map with \emph{normative references}
        to appendix registries (CT-xx tables);
  \item normatively populated but \textbf{non-executable},
        \textbf{non-procedural}, and \textbf{classification-only}
        contract registries in the appendix;
  \item a lineage statement to PDF-0, PDF-2, and PDF-3
        establishing separation of concerns
        and provenance context only.
\end{itemize}

Appendix registries included in RUN-OPS0-01:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{MAY} define identifiers, names, and declarative classifications;
  \item \textbf{MAY} bind STOP, NO-GO, and admissibility outcomes normatively;
  \item \textbf{MUST NOT} define algorithms, procedures, detection rules,
        heuristics, metrics, evaluation steps, or execution semantics;
  \item \textbf{MUST NOT} be interpretable as implementation guidance
        or operational logic.
\end{itemize}

No section of OPS-0 may introduce additional obligations, semantics,
detection logic, thresholds, metrics, requirements,
or evaluative criteria beyond the elements listed above.

Any content not explicitly listed in this subsection
\textbf{MUST NOT} be considered part of OPS-0
and is \textbf{explicitly out of scope} for this run,
regardless of interpretative convenience.

\subsection{Run Finality and Reservation}
RUN-OPS0-01 constitutes a \textbf{closed, scope-locked,
and self-contained contract artefact}.

The presence of normatively populated appendix registries
is \textbf{intentional} and \textbf{does not} imply executability,
procedural completeness, or implementation readiness.

OPS-0 explicitly disclaims:
\begin{itemize}
  \item executable detection mechanisms,
  \item validation procedures,
  \item test cases or benchmarks,
  \item schemas, formats, or rendering rules,
  \item implementation guidance of any kind.
\end{itemize}

All executable logic, detection mechanisms,
schemas, tests, examples, or tooling realizations
\textbf{MUST} be deferred to RUN-OPS0-02
or subsequent runs and have \textbf{no normative effect}
on RUN-OPS0-01.
