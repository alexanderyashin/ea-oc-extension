\section{Forbidden Operations}

\subsection{Normative Definition}
An operation is \textbf{forbidden} under OPS-0 if it violates any scope lock, non-claim, epistemic legitimacy requirement,
or crosses a STOP / NO-GO boundary as defined by this contract.

RUN-OPS0-01 \textbf{MUST NOT} provide a populated forbidden-operation list.
Forbidden status is established exclusively through normative constraints and registry bindings defined elsewhere.

\subsection{Binding to Registries}
The classification of forbidden operations is normatively bound to appendix registries (CT-xx tables),
which, in later runs, enumerate operation classes that are explicitly disallowed.
In RUN-OPS0-01, these registries are referenced for completeness but remain unpopulated.

\subsection{Precedence Rule}
In case of ambiguity or overlap, \textbf{forbidden status takes precedence} over admissibility.
If an operation is both tentatively admissible and forbidden under different constraints,
it \textbf{MUST} be treated as forbidden without exception.
