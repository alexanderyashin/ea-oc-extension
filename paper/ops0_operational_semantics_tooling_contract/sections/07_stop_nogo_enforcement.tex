\section{STOP / NO-GO Enforcement}
\label{sec:ops0_stop_nogo_enforcement}

\subsection{Terminal Epistemic Boundary}
\textbf{STOP} is the \textbf{sole terminal epistemic boundary} within OPS-0.
Once STOP is reached, the current run \textbf{MUST} be considered terminated
for all purposes of this contract.

\paragraph{Terminality is prohibitive.}
Within OPS-0, ``terminal'' is \textbf{not} merely descriptive.
It is \textbf{prohibitive}: after STOP, the governed tool/process
\textbf{MUST NOT} perform any further tool-visible operations,
transformations, evaluations, projections, or emissions.

\paragraph{Post-STOP emission ban (hard).}
After STOP, the tool/process \textbf{MUST NOT} emit any additional artifacts,
reports, figures, tables, explanatory text, auxiliary logs, downgraded
outputs, alternative results, or ``best-effort'' continuations.

The only permitted emission at the STOP boundary is the explicit
STOP artifact itself and the minimal provenance binding required by OPS-0.
No other output modality is permitted under this contract.

\paragraph{No recovery semantics.}
OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} define recovery, remediation, continuation,
optimization, control behavior, or compensatory logic following STOP.

Any behavior or artifact produced beyond STOP is \textbf{forbidden}
and \textbf{MUST} be treated as \textbf{NON-CONFORMANT} under the OPS-0
conformance matrix (\S\ref{sec:ops0_conformance_tests}).

\paragraph{Outside-contract does not mean permitted.}
Statements of the form ``outside OPS-0'' are boundary markers only.
They \textbf{MUST NOT} be interpreted as permission, allowance,
fallback authorization, or a legitimate continuation mode.

\paragraph{External witness note (non-authorizing).}
OPS-0 may acknowledge that continuation beyond STOP requires an external,
out-of-band witness and a new run.
Such an external witness:
\begin{itemize}
  \item has \textbf{no normative authority} within OPS-0;
  \item \textbf{MUST NOT} retroactively alter classifications, outcomes,
        or legitimacy judgments produced under this contract.
\end{itemize}
This acknowledgement \textbf{MUST NOT} be interpreted as authorizing any
post-STOP tool behavior under OPS-0.

\subsection{Phase Precedence (Classification Only)}
OPS-0 classifies diagnostic outcomes using the following strict
precedence order:
\[
\text{STOP} \; > \; \text{COLLAPSE} \; > \; \text{INERTIA} \; > \; \text{SUCCESS}.
\]

This ordering is a \textbf{pure classification rule}.
It \textbf{MUST NOT} be interpreted as an execution sequence,
control flow, algorithm, workflow, or temporal process.

\paragraph{Precedence is evaluated before reporting.}
If STOP applies, it \textbf{MUST} be reported and emitted as terminal
(\S\ref{sec:ops0_conformance_tests}; Appendix CT-06).
No phase label, projection, metric, or narrative may be emitted
in addition to STOP.

If multiple non-STOP phase classifications would otherwise apply,
the highest-precedence classification \textbf{MUST} be reported.
No lower-precedence classification may coexist with,
override, downgrade, or qualify a higher-precedence one.

If a conflict or ambiguity prevents a unique precedence-respecting
classification within the contract surface, the tool/process \textbf{MUST}
emit STOP and terminate.

\subsection{NO-GO Definition (Prohibited Patterns)}
\textbf{NO-GO} denotes a set of \textbf{explicitly prohibited patterns}
of tool output or tool-visible behavior under OPS-0.

NO-GO:
\begin{itemize}
  \item is \textbf{not} a phase;
  \item is \textbf{not} a terminal epistemic state;
  \item identifies behavior that is \textbf{forbidden} and \textbf{non-conformant}.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{NO-GO does not authorize fallback.}
The presence or detection of a NO-GO pattern:
\begin{itemize}
  \item does \textbf{not} authorize continuation, fallback, degraded modes,
        or compensatory outputs;
  \item does \textbf{not} replace or weaken STOP when STOP conditions apply;
  \item may co-occur with STOP, in which case STOP remains the
        \textbf{sole terminal outcome}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Binding to Registries (Exclusive Authority)}
All STOP triggers, NO-GO patterns, and classification bindings are defined
\emph{exclusively} in the Appendix contract tables (CT-xx),
which constitute the \textbf{sole authoritative registry surface}
for OPS-0.

Main sections:
\begin{itemize}
  \item may reference registry identifiers for orientation only;
  \item \textbf{MUST NOT} define, extend, populate, reinterpret,
        or operationalize registry content.
\end{itemize}

RUN-OPS0-01 \textbf{MUST NOT} define detection rules, triggers,
tests, heuristics, metrics, or enforcement mechanisms.
It specifies \emph{classification outcomes and terminal emission only}.
