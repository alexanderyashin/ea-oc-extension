\section{Output Legitimacy}

\subsection{Normative Requirement}
An output is classified as \textbf{legitimate} under OPS-0 if and only if it satisfies
\emph{all} of the following contract-level conditions:

\begin{itemize}
  \item it is traceable \textbf{exclusively} to inputs that are admissible under OPS-0;
  \item it declares an explicit \texttt{epistemic\_status} consistent with the run outcome;
  \item it embeds mandatory provenance and authorship information
        as required by this contract;
  \item it contains \textbf{no} prescriptive guidance, recommendations,
        action proposals, optimization objectives, recovery logic,
        or control semantics of any kind.
\end{itemize}

Narrative, explanatory, descriptive, or informational content
is \textbf{not} automatically legitimate by virtue of being
``non-prescriptive''.
All outputs are subject to the same legitimacy classification
regardless of modality or tone.

RUN-OPS0-01 \textbf{MUST NOT} define output formats, schemas,
validation procedures, rendering rules, or enforcement mechanisms.
Legitimacy is assessed \textbf{solely} at the contract
and classification level.

No output may claim legitimacy by implication,
context, intent, or external interpretation.

\subsection{Closed-world legitimacy rule}
OPS-0 applies a \textbf{closed-world assumption} to output legitimacy.

Any output that cannot be
\textbf{explicitly classified as legitimate}
within the declared contract surface
and by reference to the appendix registries
\textbf{MUST} be treated as \textbf{non-legitimate}.

Absence of legitimacy classification authority
\emph{constitutes illegitimacy}.
No provisional, partial, downgraded,
or best-effort legitimacy is permitted.

\subsection{Illegitimacy and STOP Classification}
If an output fails to satisfy any legitimacy condition,
or if its production would require crossing
a STOP or NO-GO boundary,
the output \textbf{MUST} be classified as \textbf{STOP}
and the run \textbf{MUST} terminate immediately.

After STOP:
\begin{itemize}
  \item no downgraded, advisory, explanatory,
        narrative, or compensatory outputs are permitted;
  \item no alternative, partial, or deferred outputs may be emitted;
  \item no recovery, continuation, or reinterpretation
        is allowed within OPS-0.
\end{itemize}

Any artefact produced beyond STOP
is \textbf{non-conformant} and
carries no normative standing
under the OPS-0 contract.

\subsection{Binding to Registries (Exclusive)}
All output legitimacy classes,
illegitimacy conditions,
and STOP classifications
are \textbf{normatively bound}
to appendix contract registries (CT-xx tables).

In RUN-OPS0-01:
\begin{itemize}
  \item registries are referenced for structural completeness only;
  \item registries \textbf{MUST NOT} be populated,
        executed, or interpreted;
  \item no legitimacy class may be inferred
        outside explicit contract
        and registry bindings.
\end{itemize}

Any output that cannot be
\textbf{explicitly classified as legitimate}
by contract and registry reference
\textbf{MUST} be treated as \textbf{non-legitimate}
under OPS-0 in this run.
