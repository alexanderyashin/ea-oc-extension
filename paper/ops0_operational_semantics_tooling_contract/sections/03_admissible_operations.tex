\section{Admissible Operations}

\subsection{Normative Definition}
Within OPS-0, an operation is considered \textbf{admissible} \emph{if and only if} it is
\textbf{explicitly classified as admissible} by reference to the applicable appendix contract registries (CT-xx)
and does not violate any scope, boundary, or non-claim constraints defined in this document.

RUN-OPS0-01 \textbf{MUST NOT} define, populate, interpret, or execute any operation registry and
\textbf{MUST NOT} assign operational, behavioral, or computational semantics to any operation.
Accordingly, admissibility in RUN-OPS0-01 is \textbf{structural and declarative only} and carries
\textbf{no executable meaning}.

\subsection{Registry Binding (Exclusive)}
All admissibility classifications for operations are \textbf{exclusively bound} to appendix contract tables (CT-xx).
These registries constitute the \emph{sole authoritative surface} for determining whether an operation class
is admissible, non-admissible, or STOP-triggering.

In RUN-OPS0-01, appendix registries are \textbf{referenced only}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item they \textbf{MUST NOT} be populated;
  \item they \textbf{MUST NOT} be evaluated;
  \item they \textbf{MUST NOT} be relied upon for execution, inference, or decision-making.
\end{itemize}

Any apparent operation naming in OPS-0 outside of registry reference has \textbf{no normative force}.

\subsection{Closed-World Scope Constraint}
OPS-0 enforces a \textbf{closed-world assumption} with respect to admissible operations.

Any tool-visible action, transformation, or behavior that is \textbf{not explicitly classified as admissible}
by an applicable CT-xx registry in a later run \textbf{MUST} be treated as \emph{non-admissible} under OPS-0.

No implicit admissibility, inference, heuristic allowance, default permission, or best-effort continuation
is permitted under this contract.
