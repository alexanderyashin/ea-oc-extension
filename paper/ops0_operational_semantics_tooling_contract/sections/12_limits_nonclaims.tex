\section{Limits and Non-Claims (Strict)}

OPS-0 (RUN-OPS0-01) is a \textbf{strictly limited}, \textbf{diagnostic-only} contract.
Accordingly, it explicitly \textbf{MUST NOT} provide, imply, or enable any of the following:

\begin{itemize}
  \item prescriptive guidance of any kind, including recommendations, action plans, remediation steps, optimization objectives, or ``how-to'' instructions;
  \item operational semantics for tools, registries, predicates, operations, admissibility rules, or enforcement mechanisms;
  \item implementations, algorithms, workflows, simulations, examples, templates, demonstrations, or reference executions;
  \item introduction of new Ontology of Continua (OC) primitives, extensions, reinterpretations, or modifications of the semantics of \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1};
  \item any weakening, bypass, deferral, recovery path, or continuation semantics that would undermine \textbf{STOP} as a terminal epistemic boundary.
\end{itemize}

OPS-0 makes \textbf{no claims} regarding effectiveness, optimality, completeness, correctness of outcomes, or suitability for decision-making, control, governance, or execution.

This artefact is:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{diagnostic-only};
  \item \textbf{scope-locked} to the contract surface defined in this document;
  \item \textbf{normatively complete} at the level of classification, validation, and enforcement boundaries;
  \item \textbf{non-operational} by design.
\end{itemize}

Any content, behavior, or interpretation that exceeds these limits is \textbf{out of scope} for OPS-0 and \textbf{MUST NOT} be attributed to this contract.
