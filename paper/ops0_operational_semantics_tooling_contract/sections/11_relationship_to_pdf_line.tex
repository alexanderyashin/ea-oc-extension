\section{Relationship to the PDF Line (Lineage and Separation)}
OPS-0 is an \textbf{orthogonal contract scaffold} relative to the PDF artifacts.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PDF-0} (KEA / foundations): defines baseline framing and artifacts; OPS-0 does not redefine them.
  \item \textbf{PDF-2} (Structural Diagnosability \& Phase Logic): diagnostic formalization; OPS-0 does not add methods or advice.
  \item \textbf{PDF-3} (next artifact in the line): remains separate; OPS-0 only states that tool-contract constraints will exist.
\end{itemize}

OPS-0 is \textbf{not} an engine, \textbf{not} a methodology, and \textbf{not} operational guidance.
It is a contract boundary document intended to constrain tooling behavior in later runs.
