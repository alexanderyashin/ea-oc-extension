\section{Relationship to the PDF Line (Lineage and Separation)}

OPS-0 is an \textbf{orthogonal contract scaffold} relative to the PDF artifact line.
It establishes tooling and operational constraints without redefining, extending,
or interpreting the content of those artifacts.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PDF-0 (KEA / foundations)}: defines baseline framing and core artifacts;
        OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} redefine or reinterpret them.
  \item \textbf{PDF-2 (Structural Diagnosability \& Phase Logic)}: provides diagnostic formalization;
        OPS-0 \textbf{MUST NOT} add methods, recommendations, or advice.
  \item \textbf{PDF-3 (subsequent artifact in the line)}: remains separate;
        OPS-0 \textbf{MAY} state that tooling contract constraints exist,
        but \textbf{MUST NOT} populate or operationalize them in this run.
\end{itemize}

OPS-0 is \textbf{not} an engine, \textbf{not} a methodology, and \textbf{not} operational guidance.
It is a boundary contract intended solely to constrain admissible tooling behavior in later runs.
