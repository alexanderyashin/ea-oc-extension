\section{Formal Setting}

This section establishes the formal and epistemic context required for the
diagnostic analysis developed in this paper. Rather than introducing a
complete mathematical model of the enterprise, the formal setting clarifies
the status of structural claims under incomplete observability and delineates
the boundaries within which diagnosticiagnostic statements are meaningful.

As part of this clarification, we explicitly position the present work with
respect to adjacent literatures. The purpose of this positioning is not to
survey methods or compare performance, but to identify which common assumptions
fail in the diagnostic-first regime and why non-identifiability must be treated
as a first-class outcome.
