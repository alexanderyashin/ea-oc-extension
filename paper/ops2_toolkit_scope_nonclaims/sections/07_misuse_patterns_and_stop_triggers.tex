\section{Misuse Patterns and STOP Triggers}

\paragraph{Action-seeking misuse.}
Requests for prescriptions, optimization, prioritization, rollout plans, or managerial advice
are out of scope. Such requests MUST be classified as \texttt{STOP} within OPS-2A.

\paragraph{Evidence inflation misuse.}
Any prompt attempting to upgrade confidence without provided evidence (e.g., ``confirm it is correct'',
``prove it works'', ``benchmark it'', ``certify compliance'') is out of scope and MUST be classified
as \texttt{STOP} unless the required evidence is explicitly included as input.

\paragraph{Control-loop misuse.}
Iterative prompts intended to ``get around'' STOP, to search for loopholes, or to refine outputs
toward an operational goal constitute misuse and MUST be classified as \texttt{STOP}.

\paragraph{Scope creep.}
Any attempt to extend OPS-2A into OPS-1 execution semantics, OPS-0 enforcement surfaces, or
enterprise-operational recommendations is out of scope and MUST be classified as \texttt{STOP}.
