\section{Conformance and STOP Integrity}

\paragraph{STOP terminality.}
STOP is treated as a first-class terminal epistemic boundary. Once STOP applies for
a declared case, no further diagnostic emission is admissible for that case.

\paragraph{Conformance-first discipline.}
Any detected misuse pattern within OPS-2A scope is treated as a conformance failure
and is classified as STOP-triggering behavior. No corrective actions, mitigations,
or recovery recommendations are permitted within OPS-2A.

\paragraph{Non-extendability.}
OPS-2A is constitutive: it defines boundaries rather than offering paths around them.
