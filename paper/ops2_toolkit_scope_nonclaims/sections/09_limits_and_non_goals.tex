\section{Limits and Non-Goals}

OPS-2A explicitly does \emph{not}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.25em]
  \item provide remediation, fixes, recommendations, or action plans;
  \item optimize processes, KPIs, or decision outcomes;
  \item generate evidence, benchmarks, audits, or validation claims;
  \item infer missing provenance, reconstruct history, or assume hidden context;
  \item function as an execution engine, workflow orchestrator, or control loop.
\end{itemize}

All such requests are classified as misuse within OPS-2A scope and are \texttt{STOP}-triggering.
