\section{Evidence and Provenance Boundary}

\paragraph{No evidence inflation.}
OPS-2A does not generate, aggregate, or upgrade evidentiary strength. Any attempt to
``manufacture'' confidence (e.g., by repeated restatement, implied benchmarking, or
optimization claims) is out of scope and constitutes misuse.

\paragraph{Provenance minimum.}
OPS-2A outputs MAY include minimal provenance sufficient for traceability, such as:
artifact identifier/version, timestamp, declared scope label, and the applied conformance
classification (OK vs. STOP). OPS-2A MUST NOT infer or fabricate missing provenance fields.

\paragraph{No external validation claims.}
OPS-2A MUST NOT claim independent verification of builds, hashes, signatures, third-party
audits, or empirical validation unless such evidence is explicitly provided as input.
