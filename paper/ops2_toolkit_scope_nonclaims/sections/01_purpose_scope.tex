\section{Purpose and Scope}

\paragraph{Purpose.}
OPS-2A defines the fixed identity of the OPS-2 toolkit as a tooling-level diagnostic
machine. Its outputs are limited to classification, constraint checks, and STOP
terminal boundary emission. OPS-2A consolidates explicit non-claims and a misuse
matrix that formally rejects out-of-scope interpretations and behaviors.

\paragraph{Scope lock (non-extendable).}
The scope of OPS-2A is closed and non-extendable. Any attempt to reinterpret or widen
OPS-2 beyond diagnostic-only semantics constitutes a scope violation.

\paragraph{Out of scope (non-exhaustive).}
OPS-2A excludes any prescriptive guidance, optimization, control logic, recovery
procedures, or managerial advice. OPS-2A does not define new enterprise semantics,
does not reinterpret ETS semantics, and does not introduce new primitives.
