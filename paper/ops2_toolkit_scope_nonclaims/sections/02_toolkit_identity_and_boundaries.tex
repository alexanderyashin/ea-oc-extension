\section{Toolkit Identity and Boundaries}

\subsection{Identity}
OPS-2 is defined as a tooling-level diagnostic machine. Its behavior is constrained
to admissibility checks, classification emission, provenance binding, and terminal
STOP enforcement.

\subsection{Lower boundary}
OPS-2 does not define or alter the underlying enterprise semantics, predicates, or
phase logic. Any semantic authority remains outside OPS-2 and cannot be introduced
through tooling.

\subsection{Upper boundary}
OPS-2 does not prescribe actions, recommend changes, optimize objectives, or provide
control-loop behavior. Any attempt to induce `you should do X'' from diagnostic
outputs is rejected.

\subsection{Category-exclusion firewall}
OPS-2 explicitly blocks prohibited categories (prescription, recovery, optimization,
control, scope extension, semantic drift) and treats them as STOP-triggering misuse
patterns under the consolidated matrix.
