\section{Relationship to OPS-0 and OPS-1}

\paragraph{Non-substitution.}
OPS-2A does not replace OPS-0 (operational semantics contract) nor OPS-1 (engine core).
OPS-2A is limited to scope labeling, non-claims, and misuse classification.

\paragraph{No reinterpretation.}
OPS-2A MUST NOT reinterpret, relax, or extend OPS-0/OPS-1 semantics. If an input requires
OPS-0/OPS-1 interpretation beyond the provided text, OPS-2A MUST classify as \texttt{STOP}.

\paragraph{Boundary consistency.}
When OPS-0/OPS-1 declare STOP as terminal, OPS-2A mirrors this as a conformance boundary:
OPS-2A may only classify, not operationalize, mitigate, or route around STOP.
