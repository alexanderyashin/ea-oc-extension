\section{Abstract}

This document defines the \emph{Engine Core} as a \textbf{diagnostic-only kernel}
within the OPS-1 layer. The Engine Core is specified as a minimal, non-adaptive,
non-intervening evaluator that assesses immutable structural state snapshots
against explicitly declared invariants and terminates evaluation irreversibly
when STOP conditions apply.

PDF-4 strictly restricts the Engine Core to diagnostic semantics.
The kernel introduces no control logic, no optimization objectives, no learning
or adaptation mechanisms, and no prescriptive, managerial, or interpretive layer.
All admissible operations are closed, explicitly declared, and structurally
bounded.

The Engine Core is defined as a formally constrained mapping from declared inputs
to declared diagnostic outputs. Its sole function is to determine structural
admissibility or non-admissibility with respect to invariants and thresholds.
STOP is treated as a first-class terminal boundary, not as an error, warning,
or recoverable condition.

This artefact provides a normative diagnostic anchor for higher-level tooling
and documents. Directionality is strictly one-way: OPS-1 definitions may be
referenced by external artefacts, while PDF-4 itself does not depend on or
inherit operational, methodological, or governance semantics.

PDF-4 does not specify implementations, execution environments, workflows,
or organizational processes. It defines the Engine Core exclusively as a
self-contained diagnostic boundary.
