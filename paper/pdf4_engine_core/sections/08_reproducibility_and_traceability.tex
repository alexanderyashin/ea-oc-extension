\section{Reproducibility and Traceability}

This section specifies the minimal reproducibility and traceability guarantees
required of an OPS-1 Engine Core artefact. These guarantees are \emph{structural}:
they concern the ability to reproduce diagnostic results from declared inputs,
not the empirical behavior of the diagnosed system.

\subsection{Evidence hooks (append-only)}

The Engine Core exposes \emph{hooks} for attaching evidence artefacts and logs,
but does not interpret, evaluate, or reason about their content.

\paragraph{Append-only rule.}
All evidence attachments must be append-only. Once referenced, no record may be
modified or deleted. This rule exists to preserve traceability, not to enforce
evidentiary sufficiency.

\paragraph{No semantic use.}
Attached evidence must not influence kernel evaluation, classification, or STOP
semantics. Any semantic use of evidence occurs outside the Engine Core boundary.

\subsection{Provenance (inputs-to-outputs mapping)}

Each diagnostic result $R$ must be traceable to its declared inputs via a minimal
provenance mapping:
\[
(S,\mathcal{I},\Theta) \;\mapsto\; R
\]

Traceability is established through stable identifiers (hashes or canonical
digests), not through explanatory narratives or causal accounts.

\paragraph{No explanatory burden.}
Provenance supports reproducibility and conformance checks only. It must not be
extended to justify decisions, explain causality, or defend outcomes.

\subsection{Hashing and manifests}

The Engine Core must expose stable identifiers for:
\begin{itemize}
  \item input state snapshots;
  \item invariant sets;
  \item threshold objects;
  \item the Engine Core version.
\end{itemize}

The choice of hashing algorithms, digest formats, or manifest encodings is
external to kernel semantics, provided identifiers are stable and inspectable.

\subsection{Run logs (minimal)}

If run logs are produced, they must be limited to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item timestamps;
  \item identifiers of inputs and outputs;
  \item STOP occurrence (if any);
  \item build or environment identifiers (optional).
\end{itemize}

Run logs are observational artefacts only. They must not encode recommendations,
interpretations, recovery logic, or procedural guidance.
