\section{Non-Appropriation and Anti-Rebranding Rules}

\subsection{Purpose (Normative)}
This section defines explicit non-appropriation and anti-rebranding rules for the OPS-2 Toolkit.
Its purpose is to prevent silent appropriation, origin erasure, and presentation of OPS-2 (or its components) as proprietary or independently originated work.

These rules serve epistemic honesty and transparency only.
They do not introduce enforcement, licensing, or governance mechanisms.

\subsection{Prohibition of Silent Appropriation (Normative)}
Any reuse, adaptation, or derivative of OPS-2 must not:
\begin{itemize}
  \item remove, obscure, or downgrade mandatory attribution,
  \item present OPS-2 concepts as independently originated,
  \item rename OPS-2 or its core semantics in a way that hides origin,
  \item imply that OPS-2 is proprietary to the reuser.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Normative statement.}
Silent appropriation of OPS-2 or its diagnostic semantics is explicitly prohibited.

\subsection{Anti-Rebranding Rules (Normative)}
Reusers must not:
\begin{itemize}
  \item rename OPS-2 as a new proprietary framework,
  \item claim authorship over OPS-2 semantics,
  \item market OPS-2-derived material as an ``in-house'' or exclusive method without attribution,
  \item present renamed OPS-2 components as original inventions.
\end{itemize}

Renaming for local clarity is permitted only if:
\begin{itemize}
  \item original terminology is referenced,
  \item attribution remains intact and visible,
  \item no originality or ownership claim is implied.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Forks, Derivatives, and Adaptations}
Forks, derivatives, and adaptations of OPS-2 are permitted provided that:
\begin{itemize}
  \item original attribution remains visible,
  \item derivative status is explicitly stated,
  \item OPS-2 origin is not obscured,
  \item no endorsement or authority transfer is implied.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Required derivative statement (Normative).}
\begin{quote}
``This work is a derivative of the OPS-2 Diagnostic Toolkit (EA-OC-EXTENSION / ESTRA).
It is independently adapted and not endorsed by the original author.''
\end{quote}

\subsection{Prohibition of Proprietary Capture}
OPS-2 must not be enclosed into proprietary claims as the original standard, relicensed as a closed or exclusive methodology, or presented as an internal corporate invention.

This restriction applies to misrepresentation of origin only.
It does not restrict proprietary implementations built on top of OPS-2.

\subsection{No Policing and No Enforcement (Explicit)}
This section defines normative expectations only.
It introduces no enforcement authority, compliance body, sanction mechanism, or dispute resolution process.

Non-conformance affects credibility and transparency, not legality.
