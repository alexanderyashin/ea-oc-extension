\section{Citation and Reuse Semantics}

\subsection{Purpose (Normative)}
This section defines how the OPS-2 Toolkit must be cited and referenced in academic, technical, and professional contexts.
Its purpose is to ensure unambiguous traceability of origin, prevent implied authority transfer, and preserve epistemic honesty.

This section introduces no licensing, approval, or validation semantics.

\subsection{Citation Requirement (Normative)}
Any work that uses OPS-2 concepts, relies on OPS-2 semantics, adapts OPS-2 structure, or references OPS-2 diagnostically must cite OPS-2 explicitly.

Omission of citation constitutes misrepresentation of origin.

\subsection{Canonical Citation Form (Normative)}
Where formal citation is possible, the following canonical reference should be used:
\begin{quote}
\emph{OPS-2 Diagnostic Toolkit — Attribution \& Signature Layer}\\
Alexander Yashin\\
EA-OC-EXTENSION / ESTRA\\
Diagnostic-only, non-prescriptive standard\\
(No endorsement implied)
\end{quote}

Minor formatting adaptations are permitted.
Semantic content must not be altered.

\subsection{Informal and Non-Academic Reuse}
In informal contexts such as internal documents, presentations, or workshops, citation must still include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the OPS-2 name,
  \item the author,
  \item the EA-OC-EXTENSION / ESTRA origin,
  \item an explicit non-endorsement notice.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Minimal acceptable form.}
\begin{quote}
``Based on OPS-2 Diagnostic Toolkit (A. Yashin, ESTRA).
No endorsement implied.''
\end{quote}

\subsection{No Authority Transfer (Critical)}
Citation of OPS-2 must not be framed as approval of results, validation of conclusions, endorsement of decisions, delegation of authority, or compliance or certification reference.

\paragraph{Normative statement.}
Citation of OPS-2 conveys reference only.
It does not transfer authority, responsibility, or credibility.

\subsection{Reuse in Composite Frameworks}
When OPS-2 is used as part of a larger or composite framework:
\begin{itemize}
  \item OPS-2 must be cited as an external diagnostic component,
  \item its role must be described as diagnostic-only,
  \item boundaries between OPS-2 and other components must be explicit.
\end{itemize}

OPS-2 must not be presented as the governing method, decision engine, or validating authority.

\subsection{No Implicit Standard Status}
Citation of OPS-2 does not imply ISO, DIN, IEEE, or regulatory status, does not establish de facto standard authority, and does not claim institutional endorsement.
