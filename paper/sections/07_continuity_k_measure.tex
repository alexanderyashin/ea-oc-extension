\section{Continuity Measure $k_{\mathrm{EA}}$}

\subsection{Meaning of Continuity in OC}

In the Ontology-of-Continua framework, continuity is not a geometric or
topological notion in the classical sense. It represents the degree of
structural connectivity among admissible states under the defined
dynamics.

For the enterprise continuum, continuity expresses whether admissible
states remain mutually reachable without violating thresholds or
breaking required cycles.

\subsection{Definition of $k_{\mathrm{EA}}$}

The continuity measure $k_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is defined as a scalar quantity
bounded in the interval $[0,1]$.

A value of $k_{\mathrm{EA}} = 1$ indicates full structural connectivity
under admissible dynamics. A value of $k_{\mathrm{EA}} = 0$ represents a
hard structural break and terminates diagnosis.

\subsection{Factorization}

According to \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}, $k_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is factorized into
the following components:

\begin{itemize}
  \item $H_{\Omega}$: indicator of non-empty admissible state space,
  \item $S_{\text{cycles}}$: contribution of required cycles,
  \item $S_{\text{obs}}$: contribution of observability,
  \item $S_{\text{parent}}$: compatibility with parent continua,
  \item $S_{\text{thresholds}}$: compliance with admissibility
        thresholds.
\end{itemize}

The aggregation rule is multiplicative. Violation of any hard factor
results in $k_{\mathrm{EA}} = 0$.

\subsection{Hard Zero Condition}

The factor $H_{\Omega}$ acts as a hard gate. If the admissible state
space becomes empty, continuity collapses immediately, regardless of
the values of other factors.

This reflects the principle that connectivity cannot exist in the
absence of admissible states.

\subsection{Continuity Versus Performance}

Continuity should not be interpreted as a measure of performance,
efficiency, or success. A highly performant enterprise may exhibit low
structural continuity, and vice versa.

The measure exists solely to determine whether diagnosis remains
structurally admissible.

\subsection{Role in Phase Logic}

The value of $k_{\mathrm{EA}}$ influences phase transitions indirectly
through its interaction with threshold components and STOP conditions.

A decline of continuity toward zero signals approaching termination of
diagnosis but does not, by itself, prescribe any action.
