\section{Invariants and No-Go Results}

\subsection{Role of Invariants}

Invariants in $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ express structural properties that hold
across all admissible states of the enterprise continuum. They are not
assumptions and not design guidelines; they are consequences of the
formal specification.

Violation of an invariant does not indicate poor performance. It
indicates that the continuum is no longer well-defined.

\subsection{Energy of Transformation}

The invariant \emph{Energy of Transformation} states that transitions
between admissible states require non-zero structural expenditure.

There are no cost-free transitions in $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$. Any apparent
instantaneous or frictionless change implies that the model’s
admissibility conditions are no longer satisfied.

This invariant forbids interpretations in which large-scale structural
change occurs without corresponding impact on thresholds, cycles, or
continuity.

\subsection{Parent Compatibility}

The invariant \emph{Parent Compatibility} requires that all admissible
enterprise states remain compatible with the constraints imposed by the
parent continuum $K_7$.

An enterprise state that violates parent compatibility is not a degraded
state of $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$; it is an inadmissible state outside
$\Omega_{\mathrm{EA}}$.

\subsection{No-Go Results}

No-Go results are formal impossibility statements derived from the ETS.
They specify transitions or interpretations that are structurally
forbidden.

\subsection{Metric Substitution}

The No-Go result \emph{Metric Substitution} forbids replacing structural
diagnosis with isolated performance metrics.

No finite set of scalar metrics can substitute for evaluation of
admissibility conditions, cycles, and continuity. Attempting to do so
invalidates diagnostic conclusions.

\subsection{Instant Recovery}

The No-Go result \emph{Instant Recovery} forbids interpretations in which
an enterprise exits COLLAPSE or STOP states without traversing admissible
transitions.

Recovery, if it occurs, must involve reconstitution of admissible state
space and cycles. Instantaneous re-entry into SUCCESS is not defined
within the model.

\subsection{No-Go Results as Guarantees of Integrity}

No-Go results do not weaken the model. They protect its internal
consistency by preventing interpretations that would otherwise
contradict executable closure or ontological grounding.
