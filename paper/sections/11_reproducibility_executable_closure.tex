\section{Reproducibility and Executable Closure}

\subsection{Meaning of Reproducibility in This Context}

Reproducibility in $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ does not refer to repeated empirical
measurement or statistical replication. It refers to the ability to
reconstruct the same formal object from the same specification without
interpretation or invention.

The object of reproduction is the structure of the continuum itself,
not observed enterprise behavior.

\subsection{Executable Closure}

The Enterprise Continuum is specified as \emph{executable-closed}. This
means that all admissible primitives, relations, constraints, and
precedence rules are contained within the immutable Executable Theory
Specification \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}.

No additional degrees of freedom are introduced by this whitepaper. The
document serves only as a projection of the ETS into human-readable
form.

\subsection{Separation of Theory and Instantiation}

The theory-level specification of $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is independent of
any concrete enterprise instantiation.

Numeric values, measurements, or observations may be supplied externally
to evaluate threshold components or continuity factors. Such values are
instantiations, not part of the theory.

This separation ensures that theoretical claims are not contaminated by
data-dependent assumptions.

\subsection{Implications for Tooling}

Executable closure implies that any tooling built on top of
\texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1} must respect the same admissibility conditions
and termination rules.

Tooling may automate evaluation, but it cannot extend or override the
theory. In particular, no tool may bypass STOP conditions or No-Go
results without leaving the scope of the model.

\subsection{Structural Falsifiability}

The model is falsifiable in a structural sense. If an enterprise is
claimed to satisfy all admissibility conditions while violating an
invariant or No-Go result, the claim is inconsistent with the theory.

This form of falsifiability does not depend on empirical calibration. It
depends on logical consistency with the executable specification.

\subsection{Role of This Whitepaper}

This whitepaper contributes to reproducibility by making the structure
of $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ explicit and inspectable. It does not provide
reference implementations, datasets, or benchmarks.

Such artifacts, if they exist, belong outside the scope of this
document.
