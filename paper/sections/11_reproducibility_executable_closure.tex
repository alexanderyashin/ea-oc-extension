\section{Reproducibility and Executable Closure}

\subsection{Meaning of Reproducibility in This Context}

Reproducibility in the context of $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ does not refer to
repeated empirical measurement, statistical replication, experimental
repeatability, or observational convergence.

It refers exclusively to the ability to reconstruct the same formal
object from the same specification without interpretation, extension, or
invention.

The object of reproduction is the structure of the enterprise continuum
itself, as defined by its primitives, admissibility conditions,
thresholds, cycles, and phase logic.
Observed enterprise behavior or outcomes are explicitly not objects of
reproduction within this model.

\subsection{Executable Closure}

The enterprise continuum $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is specified as
\emph{executable-closed}.
All admissible primitives, relations, constraints, precedence rules, and
termination conditions are contained within the immutable Executable
Theory Specification \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}.

This whitepaper introduces no additional degrees of freedom.
It serves solely as a faithful projection of the ETS into a
human-readable form.

Any interpretation, inference, or extension that relies on elements not
present in the ETS lies outside the model and is not attributable to
$K_{\mathrm{EA}}$.

\subsection{Separation of Theory and Instantiation}

The theory-level specification of $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is independent of
any concrete enterprise instantiation.

Numerical values, measurements, or observations may be supplied
externally to evaluate threshold components or continuity factors.
Such values constitute instantiations of the theory, not part of its
definition.

This separation ensures that theoretical claims remain invariant under
changes of data, measurement resolution, instrumentation, or
observational perspective.

\subsection{Implications for Tooling}

Executable closure implies that any tooling constructed on top of
\texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1} must operate strictly within the same admissible
space.

Tools may automate evaluation, inspection, or visualization.
They may not extend, override, reinterpret, or bypass admissibility
conditions, phase precedence rules, STOP states, or No-Go results.

Any such extension constitutes departure from the model rather than an
alternative implementation.
Tool output carries no authority beyond consistency with the executable
specification.

\subsection{Structural Falsifiability}

$K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is falsifiable in a structural sense.
If an enterprise is claimed to satisfy all admissibility conditions
while simultaneously violating an invariant, compatibility constraint,
or No-Go result, the claim is inconsistent with the theory.

This form of falsifiability does not depend on empirical calibration,
statistical significance, or probabilistic confidence.
It depends solely on logical consistency with the executable
specification.

\subsection{Role of This Whitepaper}

This whitepaper contributes to reproducibility by making the formal
structure of $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ explicit, inspectable, and reviewable.

It does not provide reference implementations, datasets, benchmarks, or
empirical case material.
Such artifacts, if produced, lie outside the scope of this document and
do not modify, validate, or extend the theory it presents.
