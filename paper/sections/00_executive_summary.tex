\section{Executive Summary}

\subsection*{Purpose of this Whitepaper}

This whitepaper presents a formal, diagnostic-only specification of the
\emph{Enterprise Continuum} $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$, defined within the
Ontology-of-Continua (OC) framework and projected directly from the
immutable Executable Theory Specification
\texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}.

The document exists to make the structure, limits, and admissibility
conditions of enterprise diagnosis explicit and reviewable.
It is not a proposal, a method description, or a transformation guide.
No new primitives, operators, or parameters are introduced beyond those
already defined in the underlying ETS and OC Core.

\subsection*{Problem Context}

Enterprises are commonly analyzed using frameworks that assume the
continued existence, recoverability, and transformability of the system
under study. In practice, many initiatives fail because these
assumptions silently break: structural coherence is lost, observability
collapses, or essential cycles cease to exist.

Conventional approaches lack a formal mechanism to state when diagnosis
itself becomes undefined. As a result, analysis often continues beyond
the domain where its conclusions remain meaningful.

$K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ addresses this gap by treating the enterprise as a
\emph{continuum with explicit existence conditions}. Diagnosis is
permitted only while these conditions hold.

\subsection*{Ontological Position}

$K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is defined as an OC-continuum characterized by:

\begin{itemize}
  \item a bounded state space $\Omega_{\mathrm{EA}}$ with boundary
        $\partial\Omega_{\mathrm{EA}}$,
  \item a finite set of axes $A_{\mathrm{EA}}$ representing admissible
        degrees of freedom,
  \item potentials $P_{\mathrm{EA}}$ and flows $J_{\mathrm{EA}}$
        governing internal dynamics,
  \item threshold functions $\Theta_{\mathrm{EA}}$ defining structural
        admissibility,
  \item required recurrent cycles $C_{\mathrm{EA}}$,
  \item a continuity measure $k_{\mathrm{EA}} \in [0,1]$ expressing
        structural connectivity over time.
\end{itemize}

All primitives are inherited from OC Core and instantiated strictly
according to \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}. The whitepaper does not reinterpret
or extend them.

\subsection*{Diagnostic-Only Scope}

The scope of $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is strictly diagnostic.

The model does not:
\begin{itemize}
  \item recommend actions or interventions,
  \item optimize outcomes,
  \item propose roadmaps or maturity paths,
  \item guarantee recovery or success.
\end{itemize}

All statements are structural. All outcomes are conditional.
Where the conditions for diagnosis are violated, the model terminates
analysis.

\subsection*{STOP as a First-Class Outcome}

A central feature of $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is the explicit recognition of
\emph{STOP} as a valid diagnostic result.

STOP occurs when existence, observability, or structural compatibility
conditions are violated. In this state, further diagnosis is undefined
within the model. STOP does not indicate analytical failure; it indicates
that the object of analysis no longer satisfies the minimum conditions
required to be treated as a continuum.

This treatment contrasts with conventional approaches that implicitly
assume infinite recoverability.

\subsection*{Executable Closure and Reproducibility}

$K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is specified as \emph{executable-closed}. All admissible
concepts and relations are contained within the ETS. No hidden degrees
of freedom exist outside the specification.

As a result:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the model can be reconstructed from the ETS without
        interpretation,
  \item reproducibility is structural rather than empirical,
  \item numeric values, if supplied externally, are instantiations, not
        part of the theory.
\end{itemize}

This whitepaper therefore serves as a faithful, inspectable projection
of a closed formal object.

\subsection*{Audience and Use}

This document is intended for senior enterprise architects, systems
engineers, and technical reviewers who require a precise understanding
of what an enterprise diagnostic model \emph{can} and \emph{cannot}
assert.

The whitepaper is designed to stand on its own, without accompanying
presentation or interpretation.
