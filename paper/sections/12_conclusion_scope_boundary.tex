\section{Conclusion and Scope Boundary}

\subsection{Summary of the Contribution}

This whitepaper has presented a reviewer-grade, executable-closed, and
diagnostic-only specification of the Enterprise Continuum
$K_{\mathrm{EA}}$, projected directly and without modification from the
immutable Executable Theory Specification \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}.

Specifically, the document has:
\begin{itemize}
  \item positioned enterprise diagnosis within the Ontology of Continua,
  \item defined the enterprise as a conditional and bounded continuum,
  \item formalized admissibility, persistence, and termination
        conditions,
  \item treated STOP as a first-class diagnostic outcome,
  \item preserved invariants and No-Go results as structural integrity
        constraints.
\end{itemize}

No new primitives, parameters, interpretations, assumptions, or claims
have been introduced.

\subsection{What This Whitepaper Enables}

Within its explicitly defined scope, this whitepaper enables:
\begin{itemize}
  \item structurally consistent enterprise diagnosis,
  \item explicit identification of regimes in which diagnosis is
        undefined,
  \item rigorous distinction between performance degradation and
        structural failure,
  \item inspection and formal review of diagnostic assumptions and
        boundary conditions.
\end{itemize}

All enabled capabilities arise solely from the formal structure of the
enterprise continuum as specified by the Executable Theory Specification.

\subsection{What This Whitepaper Explicitly Forbids}

This document explicitly forbids:
\begin{itemize}
  \item prescriptive or normative interpretation of diagnostic results,
  \item optimization, improvement, recovery, or transformation guidance,
  \item substitution of structural diagnosis with isolated performance
        metrics or maturity labels,
  \item bypassing STOP conditions, invariants, compatibility constraints,
        or No-Go results.
\end{itemize}

Any such use constitutes an interpretation outside the admissible scope
of the model and is not attributable to $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$.

\subsection{Boundary of Applicability}

The applicability of $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is strictly bounded by the
conditions under which an enterprise can be treated as a diagnosable
continuum.

When existence, observability, persistence, continuity, or parent
compatibility conditions are violated, diagnosis terminates.
The model makes no claims beyond this boundary and provides no
extrapolation outside it.

\subsection{Final Remark}

The Enterprise Continuum is not a promise of success, recovery, control,
or governance effectiveness.
It is a formal object designed to preserve diagnostic integrity by
making its own limits explicit.

Within those limits, it provides a coherent and reproducible basis for
enterprise diagnosis.
Beyond them, it deliberately remains silent.
