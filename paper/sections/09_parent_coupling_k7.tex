\section{Parent Coupling: Constraints from the K7 Continuum}

\subsection{Non-Isolated Nature of the Enterprise Continuum}

The enterprise continuum $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ does not exist in isolation.
It is embedded within a parent continuum, denoted as $K_7$, which
represents the broader socio-technical environment in which the
enterprise operates.

Parent coupling does not prescribe behavior. It constrains admissibility
by shaping the external conditions under which $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ may
exist and remain diagnosable.

\subsection{External Fields}

According to \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1}, the following external fields are
recognized as relevant couplings from $K_7$:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Law}: legal existence, liability, and enforceability.
  \item \textbf{Regulation}: sector-specific and cross-sector
        constraints.
  \item \textbf{Market}: demand, competition, and economic pressure.
  \item \textbf{Technology}: available technical substrate and limits.
  \item \textbf{Norms}: social and organizational expectations.
\end{itemize}

These fields act as exogenous constraints on the admissible state space
of $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$.

\subsection{Effects on Enterprise Primitives}

External fields influence the enterprise continuum indirectly through
their effects on existing primitives:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Law and regulation constrain existence and termination
        thresholds.
  \item Market conditions affect resource-related potentials and inertia
        thresholds.
  \item Technology constrains structural and informational axes.
  \item Norms affect observability and semantic alignment.
\end{itemize}

No external field is permitted to directly control enterprise axes or
internal dynamics.

\subsection{Compatibility Constraints}

The following compatibility constraints are enforced by the ETS:

\begin{itemize}
  \item $\Omega_{\mathrm{EA}} \subseteq \Omega_{7}$: the enterprise state
        space must remain a subset of the parent continuum's admissible
        space.
  \item No direct external axis control: parent fields cannot set values
        of $A_{\mathrm{EA}}$.
  \item No direct $K_8 \rightarrow K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ coupling: higher
        continua may influence $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ only through $K_7$.
\end{itemize}

Violation of these constraints triggers termination conditions.

\subsection{Interpretation Limits}

Parent coupling explains how external pressures may invalidate enterprise
diagnosis. It does not explain why such pressures arise, nor does it
propose mitigation strategies.

The model remains diagnostic and non-prescriptive at all levels.
