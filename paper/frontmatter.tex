\noindent\textbf{Scope declaration.}
This document is a formal, executable-closed projection of an immutable
Executable Theory Specification (ETS).
It introduces no new primitives, parameters, thresholds, operators, or
claims beyond those defined in \texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1} and Ontology of
Continua Core.

\vspace{2mm}

\noindent\textbf{Diagnostic-only commitment.}
All statements in this document are structural and diagnostic.
No prescriptive guidance, optimization objectives, roadmaps, maturity
models, or recommendations are provided or implied.

\vspace{2mm}

\noindent\textbf{Explicit non-claims.}
This whitepaper makes no claims regarding:
(i) empirical validation,
(ii) predictive accuracy,
(iii) universal numerical thresholds,
(iv) cross-enterprise comparability,
(v) recoverability, resilience, or success guarantees.

\vspace{2mm}

\noindent\textbf{STOP as a first-class outcome.}
Termination of diagnosis (STOP) is an admissible and expected result
when structural existence, observability, or compatibility conditions
are violated.
STOP indicates that further diagnosis is undefined within the model,
not that analysis has failed.

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent\textbf{Verifiability and integrity anchor (not authority).}
The ETS SHA-256 value above is an integrity anchor for \emph{file identity}.
It enables reviewers to verify that a given copy of
\texttt{ETS.K\_EA.v1.1} matches the referenced immutable source.
It does not assert empirical correctness, scientific validation, or
external authority.
Executable closure means that the formal object can be reconstructed
without interpretive degrees of freedom; it does not imply predictive
status.

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent\textbf{Reviewer orientation (boundary).}
This document is designed to be evaluated as:
(i) a closed formal object (ETS) and its faithful human-readable
projection (whitepaper),
(ii) a diagnostic boundary specification (including STOP),
(iii) a scope-locked non-prescriptive artifact.
Requests for empirical validation, case studies, prescriptive guidance,
or optimization are outside the intended document type and are not
treated as missing content.

\vspace{4mm}

\noindent\textbf{Intended readership.}
This document is intended for senior enterprise architects, systems
engineers, and technical reviewers evaluating the internal coherence,
limits, and admissibility conditions of a structural diagnostic model.
No prior exposure to ESTRA, OC, or $K_{\mathrm{EA}}$ is assumed.
